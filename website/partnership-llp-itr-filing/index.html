Overview

What is ITR filing for a partnership firm or LLP?

Partnership firms and LLPs file their income tax return in ITR-5, every year, even with a loss. A firm on presumptive taxation can use ITR-4. Income is taxed at a flat 30% plus surcharge and cess. For FY 2025-26 the return is due by 31 August 2026, or 21 November 2026 for audit cases.

The firm’s return decides how much tax is paid on the business, and how partners are taxed on what they draw from it.

Returns for FY 2025-26 are filed under Section 139(1) of the Income-tax Act, 1961. LLPs also file Form 8 and Form 11 with the MCA under the LLP Act, 2008.

Example · Trading firm

A Rajkot hardware firm with two working partners earns ₹20 lakh before partner payments. It pays 12% interest on ₹25 lakh capital (₹3 lakh), leaving ₹17 lakh book profit. The remuneration limit is ₹5.4 lakh + 60% of ₹11 lakh = ₹12 lakh, so the full ₹10 lakh planned is allowed, and tax is paid on just ₹7 lakh.

Partners

Partner remuneration, interest and TDS

  1. Remuneration (Section 40(b)): only to working partners, as the deed allows. On the first ₹6 lakh of book profit or a loss, the higher of ₹3 lakh or 90%; 60% on the rest.
  2. Interest on capital: up to 12% simple interest a year, if the deed authorises it.
  3. TDS (Section 194T): 10% on partner salary, remuneration, commission, bonus or interest above ₹20,000 a year, from 1 April 2025.
  4. Partner’s return: remuneration and interest are taxed as business income, usually in ITR-3. The profit share is exempt under Section 10(2A).
The deed decides

If the deed doesn’t specify remuneration or interest, or the method of computing it, no deduction is allowed. Update the deed before the year starts if partner terms change.

LLP filings

LLP annual filings and audits

  • Form 11 (annual return): by 30 May, with partner and contribution details.
  • Form 8 (statement of account and solvency): by 30 October, signed by designated partners.
  • Late fee: ₹100 a day for each form, with no upper limit.
  • Statutory audit (LLP Act): required if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh; Form 8 is then certified by the auditor.
  • Tax audit (Income-tax Act): separate, based on the Section 44AB turnover limits.

File on time to keep your losses (Section 80)

Business losses can be carried forward for 8 years, but only if the return for the loss year is filed by the due date. Firms must file even in a loss year.

Watch out

If your firm or LLP misses a deadline

  • Late ITR fee: up to ₹5,000 under Section 234F.
  • Interest: 1% a month or part on unpaid tax from the due date (Section 234A).
  • Losses: business losses of the year can’t be carried forward from a late return.
  • Tax audit: a missed audit can attract a penalty of up to ₹1.5 lakh (Section 271B).
  • LLP forms: ₹100 a day for each late Form 8 or Form 11, with no cap.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

Which ITR form does a partnership firm or LLP file?

Both file ITR-5. A partnership firm (other than an LLP) that opts for presumptive taxation under Section 44AD or 44ADA can file ITR-4 instead. An LLP always files ITR-5.

Is it mandatory for a firm or LLP to file an income tax return?

Yes. Every partnership firm and LLP must file its return every year under Section 139(1), whatever its income, even if it made a loss or had no business during the year.

What is the due date for ITR-5 for FY 2025-26?

The due date is 31 August 2026 for firms and LLPs that do not need a tax audit, and 21 November 2026 for tax-audit cases, extended from 31 October by CBDT Circular 7/2026. Transfer-pricing cases file by 30 November 2026.

What is the income tax rate for a partnership firm or LLP?

Firms and LLPs pay a flat 30% on taxable income, plus a 12% surcharge if income exceeds ₹1 crore, and 4% health and education cess. That is an effective rate of 31.2%, or 34.944% above ₹1 crore. There is no basic exemption limit.

How much remuneration can a firm pay its partners?

Under Section 40(b), remuneration to working partners is deductible only if the partnership deed authorises it. On the first ₹6 lakh of book profit, or in case of a loss, the limit is the higher of ₹3 lakh or 90% of book profit. On the balance, it is 60% of book profit.

How much interest on capital can partners get?

Interest on partners' capital is deductible up to 12% simple interest a year, if the partnership deed authorises it. Any interest above 12% is disallowed in the firm's hands.

Does a firm have to deduct TDS on partner remuneration?

Yes. From 1 April 2025, Section 194T requires a firm or LLP to deduct TDS at 10% on salary, remuneration, commission, bonus or interest paid or credited to a partner when the total exceeds ₹20,000 in the financial year.

How are partners taxed on income from the firm?

A partner's share of the firm's profit is exempt under Section 10(2A), because the firm has already paid tax on it. Remuneration and interest received from the firm, to the extent allowed to the firm, are taxed in the partner's hands as business income, usually in ITR-3.

When does a firm or LLP need a tax audit?

A tax audit under Section 44AB applies when turnover exceeds ₹1 crore, or ₹10 crore if cash receipts and payments are each no more than 5%, and when professional receipts exceed ₹50 lakh. Separately, an LLP needs a statutory audit under the LLP Act if its turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh.

What annual filings does an LLP have with the MCA?

An LLP files Form 11, its annual return, by 30 May, and Form 8, its statement of account and solvency, by 30 October each year. Each form carries an additional fee of ₹100 a day if filed late, so these are separate from the income tax return.

Is a digital signature required to file ITR-5?

An LLP must file ITR-5 with the digital signature (DSC) of a designated partner. A firm that needs a tax audit must also use a DSC. A firm without an audit can verify the return electronically with EVC.

Does the Income-tax Act, 2025 change ITR filing for firms and LLPs for FY 2025-26?

Returns for FY 2025-26 are filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to income from tax year 2026-27, and renumbers the provisions on presumptive taxation, tax audit and partner payments, so plan next year's books with the new references.
