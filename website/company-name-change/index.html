Overview

How does a company name change work?

To change a company’s name, reserve the new name through RUN, pass a special resolution, file it in MGT-14 within 30 days and apply to the Registrar in INC-24. The Registrar issues a fresh certificate of incorporation. The company, its PAN, GSTIN and contracts stay the same; only the name changes.

A name change is a rebrand on paper and in law. Done right, customers, banks and the tax department all follow without friction.

It is governed by Section 13 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 29 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014. LLPs follow Section 13 of the LLP Act, 2008.

Name rules

What a new name can and can’t be

  • Not identical or too similar to an existing company, LLP or registered trademark.
  • Correct ending: “Private Limited”, “(OPC) Private Limited”, “Limited” or “LLP”.
  • Restricted words need approval: Bank, Insurance, Stock Exchange, Mutual Fund, Board, Commission, Authority, National, Union, Central, Federal, Republic.
  • Not undesirable: offensive words or names that suggest a link with the government.
  • No filing defaults: annual returns and financial statements must be up to date (Rule 29).

File everything pending first (Rule 29)

The Registrar won’t approve a name change while annual returns or financial statements are pending, or deposits are in default. We check your MCA record before reserving the name, so you don’t lose the 60-day window.

LLP

Changing the name of an LLP

  1. Reserve the name through RUN-LLP.
  2. Partners’ consent as required by the LLP agreement.
  3. Form 5 for the change of name, within 30 days of the consent.
  4. Form 3 for the change in the LLP agreement, within 30 days.
  5. Fresh certificate of incorporation in the new name.

Running an LLP? See LLP Compliance for Form 8 and Form 11.

After approval

What to update after the name change

  • PAN and TAN: change request with the new certificate; numbers stay the same.
  • GST: core amendment for the legal name within 15 days. See GST Amendment.
  • Bank accounts: letter with the new certificate and board resolution.
  • Udyam, IEC, shop and trade licences: update each registration.
  • Stationery and website: show the new name with “formerly known as” for two years (Section 12).
Tax records

Name change and your tax records

A name change doesn’t create a new taxpayer. The company keeps its PAN, TAN and GSTIN, and its returns continue as before, under the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

How do I change the name of a private limited company?

Hold a board meeting, reserve the new name through RUN on the MCA portal, pass a special resolution at a general meeting, file it in MGT-14 within 30 days, and then apply to the Registrar in Form INC-24. Once approved, the Registrar issues a fresh certificate of incorporation in Form INC-25 with the new name.

How long does a company name change take?

It usually takes three to five weeks: about a week for name approval through RUN, the notice period for the general meeting, and one to two weeks for the Registrar to approve INC-24, assuming the forms are not sent back for resubmission.

How long is a reserved name valid for a name change?

A name approved through RUN for changing the name of an existing company is reserved for 60 days from approval. INC-24 must be filed within that time, or the name has to be applied for again.

Can every company change its name?

No. Under Rule 29 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, a company cannot change its name if it has not filed its annual returns or financial statements, or has defaulted in repaying deposits or interest on them, until the default is made good.

Does the company get a new CIN after a name change?

The company remains the same legal entity, with the same assets, liabilities, contracts and PAN number. The Registrar issues a fresh certificate of incorporation in the new name; the CIN may change only if the change also affects its type or listing status.

Is a special resolution required for a name change?

Yes. Under Section 13 of the Companies Act, the memorandum's name clause is changed by a special resolution of the shareholders, passed with at least three-fourths of the votes cast, and filed with the Registrar in MGT-14 within 30 days.

Which words need special approval in a company name?

Words such as Bank, Insurance, Stock Exchange, Mutual Fund, Board, Commission, Authority, National, Union, Central, Federal and Republic need approval or a no-objection from the Central Government or the relevant regulator, such as the RBI, SEBI or IRDAI, before they can be used.

How do I change the name of an LLP?

Reserve the new name through RUN-LLP, obtain the consent of the partners as required by the LLP agreement, file Form 5 for the change of name within 30 days of the consent, and file Form 3 for the change in the LLP agreement. The Registrar then issues a fresh certificate of incorporation.

What must be updated after a company name change?

Update the PAN and TAN records, apply for a GST amendment within 15 days, and inform the bank, the Udyam, IEC and shop registrations, trademarks, licences, customers and vendors. Change the name on the seal, letterheads, invoices, signboard and website.

Do I have to show the old name after the change?

Yes. Under Section 12 of the Companies Act, for two years after the change, the company must show its former name along with its new name on its signboard, letterheads, invoices and other official documents.

Can the Registrar order a company to change its name?

Yes. Under Section 16, if a name is identical or too similar to an existing company or a registered trademark, the Central Government can direct the company to change it, usually within three months of the direction.

Does a company name change affect income tax or GST registration?

The PAN and GSTIN stay the same, but the name on them must be updated. A change of legal name in GST is a core amendment that needs officer approval, and the PAN record is corrected through the PAN change request.
