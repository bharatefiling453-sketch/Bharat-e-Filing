Overview

How do you close an LLP?

An LLP that has not carried on business for a year, with nil assets and liabilities, applies to the Registrar in Form 24 to strike off its name, after filing pending Form 8 and Form 11 and closing GST and its bank account. A solvent LLP with assets uses voluntary liquidation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code instead.

An idle LLP still owes two filings every year. Closing it properly ends the cost and the risk of penalties.

Closure is governed by Sections 63 to 65 and 75 of the LLP Act, 2008, Rule 37 of the LLP Rules, 2009 and Section 59 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.

Form 24

Strike off in Form 24, step by step

  1. Stop business and settle every asset and liability to nil.
  2. File pending Form 8 and Form 11 up to the year business stopped.
  3. Close GST, TAN and the bank account, and file the final ITR-5.
  4. File Form 24 with partners’ consent, statement of accounts, affidavit and indemnity bond.
  5. Public notice by the Registrar; the name is struck off if no objection is upheld.

Don’t just abandon the LLP

An idle LLP that isn’t closed keeps owing Form 8 and Form 11 every year, each at ₹100 a day when late. Five years of default can also lead to winding up by the Tribunal. Closing early is cheaper.

Liquidation

Voluntary liquidation for solvent LLPs

  1. Declaration of solvency by a majority of designated partners.
  2. Partners’ resolution to wind up and appoint an insolvency professional as liquidator.
  3. Notices to the Registrar, the IBBI and creditors.
  4. Liquidator sells assets, pays creditors and distributes the rest.
  5. Tribunal order dissolving the LLP, filed with the Registrar.
Tribunal

When the Tribunal winds up an LLP

  • Fewer than two partners for more than six months.
  • Unable to pay its debts.
  • Five consecutive years of default in filing returns or statements.
  • Just and equitable grounds, or acting against India’s interests.

Keeping the LLP running instead? See LLP Form 11 Filing.

Tax

Closing tax and GST before Form 24

File the LLP’s final ITR-5 under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, and pay any tax due. Cancel GST in REG-16 with the final return, and surrender the TAN after the last TDS return.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

How can an LLP be closed?

There are three main routes: striking off the LLP's name by applying to the Registrar in Form 24 under Section 75 of the LLP Act and Rule 37 of the LLP Rules; voluntary liquidation of a solvent LLP under Section 59 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016; and winding up by the Tribunal under Section 64 of the LLP Act.

Who can apply for strike off in Form 24?

An LLP that has not carried on any business since incorporation, or has stopped carrying on business for at least one year, can apply with the consent of its partners, provided it has no assets or liabilities and has closed its accounts and registrations.

Do pending Form 8 and Form 11 need to be filed before closure?

Yes. The LLP must file its pending annual returns and statements of account up to the end of the financial year in which it stopped carrying on business, with the late fee of ₹100 a day for each late form.

What documents are needed for Form 24?

The partners' consent, a statement of accounts showing nil assets and liabilities certified by a chartered accountant, an affidavit and indemnity bond from the designated partners, proof of closure of the bank account, and copies of any closure of tax registrations.

How long does LLP strike off take?

Usually three to six months after Form 24 is filed. Since 27 August 2024, Form 24 applications are processed centrally, and the Registrar publishes a notice inviting objections before striking off the name.

What is voluntary liquidation of an LLP?

A solvent LLP that has assets can be wound up voluntarily under Section 59 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The designated partners declare solvency, the partners pass a resolution, an insolvency professional is appointed as liquidator to sell the assets and pay creditors, and the Tribunal passes the dissolution order.

When does the Tribunal wind up an LLP?

Under Section 64 of the LLP Act, the Tribunal can wind up an LLP if, for example, it has fewer than two partners for more than six months, it cannot pay its debts, it has defaulted in filing its returns for five consecutive years, or it acted against the interests of India.

Can an LLP with GST registration be closed?

Yes, but the GST registration should be cancelled first by applying in REG-16 and filing the final return, and the TAN should be surrendered. Strike off is easier once every registration is closed.

What happens to the partners' liability after closure?

Partners and designated partners remain liable for any liability or fraud from the period before closure. Closure does not wipe out past obligations.

Can a struck-off LLP be restored?

Yes. An aggrieved person, including a partner or creditor, can apply to the Tribunal to restore the LLP's name, generally within three years of the strike off.

What are the late fees for pending LLP filings?

Form 8 and Form 11 each carry a late fee of ₹100 a day from their due dates. An LLP that missed several years should calculate these before deciding on the closure route.

Does the LLP need to file a final income tax return?

Yes. The LLP files its income tax return in ITR-5 for the final year under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, and settles any tax due before closure.
