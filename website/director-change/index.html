Overview

How do you change a company’s directors?

To appoint a director, take their consent in DIR-2 and pass a board or shareholders’ resolution. A director resigns by written notice; shareholders remove one by ordinary resolution after special notice. In every case the company files DIR-12 with the Registrar within 30 days.

Your board is who signs, decides and answers for the company. Keep the record current and nothing is held up.

It is governed by Sections 149 to 170 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014. LLPs follow Section 25 of the LLP Act, 2008.

Example · Co-founder joins

“Iyer Foods Private Limited” in Chennai appoints a co-founder as an additional director at a board meeting on 2 November. DIR-2 and DIR-8 are signed the same day, DIR-12 is filed on 6 November, and the shareholders regularise the appointment at the next AGM.

Resignation

When a director resigns

  • Written notice to the company; effective from receipt or the date in it, whichever is later.
  • Board notes it and checks the minimum number of directors still holds.
  • DIR-12 by the company within 30 days of receiving the notice.
  • DIR-11 by the director, optional, within 30 days, with reasons.
  • Liability for offences during the tenure continues after resignation.
Good to know

Rules for every board

What every change must keep in place.

  1. Minimum

    Two, three or one

    Private two, public three, OPC one. Maximum 15 unless shareholders approve more.

  2. Resident

    One resident director Always

    At least one director who stayed in India 182 days or more in the financial year.

  3. DIN

    Active DIN for everyone KYC done

    A DIN deactivated for missed KYC must be reactivated before the director signs.

Never let the board fall short (Section 149)

Before a resignation or removal, check the board keeps its minimum number of directors and one resident director. Appoint the replacement first if needed, so the company can keep holding valid board meetings.

Removal

Removing a director under Section 169

  1. Special notice of the resolution reaches the company at least 14 days before the meeting.
  2. Copy to the director, who can make a written representation and be heard at the meeting.
  3. General meeting passes an ordinary resolution.
  4. DIR-12 within 30 days; a replacement can be appointed at the same meeting.

Directors appointed by the Tribunal under Section 242 can’t be removed this way.

LLP

Changing a designated partner in an LLP

  • Consent of the partners as the LLP agreement requires, and the new partner’s consent.
  • Form 4 within 30 days of the appointment or cessation.
  • Form 3 if the LLP agreement changes.
  • See LLP Compliance for Form 8 and Form 11.
Tax records

Director change and your tax records

The company’s PAN and TAN don’t change. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, the company’s return is still verified by its managing director, or a director where there is none, so update who signs.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

How do I add a new director to a private limited company?

Get the new director's consent in DIR-2 and declaration in DIR-8, make sure they hold an active DIN, and pass a board resolution appointing them as an additional director, or appoint them at a general meeting. The company then files DIR-12 with the Registrar within 30 days of the appointment.

How long can an additional director hold office?

Under Section 161(1), an additional director appointed by the board holds office up to the next annual general meeting, or the last date on which it should have been held, whichever is earlier. To continue, the director must be regularised by the shareholders at a general meeting.

Which form is filed for a change in directors?

DIR-12 is filed by the company within 30 days of any appointment, resignation, removal or change in designation of a director, with the consent, resolution or resignation letter attached.

How does a director resign?

The director gives a written notice of resignation to the company under Section 168. The company notes it at the board meeting and files DIR-12 within 30 days. The resignation takes effect from the date the company receives the notice or the date mentioned in it, whichever is later.

What is DIR-11?

DIR-11 is an optional form a resigning director may file with the Registrar within 30 days of resigning, giving the reasons. It is useful when the director wants an independent record of the resignation.

How can shareholders remove a director?

Under Section 169, a director can be removed by an ordinary resolution at a general meeting. A special notice of the resolution must be given at least 14 days before the meeting, and the director has the right to be heard. DIR-12 is filed within 30 days of removal.

How many directors must a company have?

Under Section 149, a private company needs at least two directors, a public company three and a one person company one, with a maximum of 15 unless more are approved by special resolution. At least one director must have stayed in India for 182 days or more in the financial year.

What if all the directors resign?

Under Section 168(3), if all directors vacate office, the promoters, or failing them the Central Government, appoint the required number of directors, who hold office until directors are appointed at a general meeting.

Does the new director need a DIN?

Yes. Every director of a company needs an active DIN. If the person does not have one, it is applied for before or along with the appointment, and an existing DIN must not be deactivated for missing KYC.

What is the penalty for filing DIR-12 late?

DIR-12 filed after 30 days attracts additional fees that rise with the delay. Not filing at all can also lead to penalties for the company and its officers in default.

Does a director change need a GST update?

Yes. Adding or removing a director responsible for the company's affairs is a core amendment in the GST registration, applied for within 15 days of the change and approved by the GST officer.

How is a designated partner changed in an LLP?

The partners consent to the change as the LLP agreement requires, and the LLP files Form 4 within 30 days of the appointment or cessation. Form 3 is also filed if the LLP agreement changes.
