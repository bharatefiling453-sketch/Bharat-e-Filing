Overview

What are revised and updated returns?

A revised return (Section 139(5)) corrects any mistake in an ITR you filed, until 31 March 2027 for FY 2025-26. An updated return (ITR-U) (Section 139(8A)) lets you report missed income up to 48 months after the assessment year ends, by paying tax, interest and 25% to 70% additional tax.

Both let you set the record straight yourself, which always costs less than the department finding it first.

A revised return replaces the original completely. An updated return adds to it, and can be filed only once for each assessment year.

Sneha, a teacher in Nagpur, forgot a short-term gain on shares in her AY 2025-26 return. The tax and interest come to ₹30,000. Filing ITR-U before 31 March 2027 adds 25%, ₹7,500. Waiting past that date would double it to ₹15,000.

Arjun, an engineer in Gurugram, forgot his parents’ health insurance under Section 80D in his FY 2025-26 return. He files a revised return before 31 December 2026 and gets a higher refund. ITR-U couldn’t have done this.

Watch out

When an updated return can’t be filed

  • It would show a loss, reduce tax or increase a refund
  • A search, survey or requisition has begun for that year
  • An assessment or reassessment is pending or completed for that year
  • Prosecution has started, or the department has information under certain laws
  • After 36 months, if a reassessment show cause notice was issued (unless the officer found it not a fit case)

Pay before you file (Section 140B)

An updated return is valid only if the tax, interest, late fee and additional tax are paid before it is filed. An ITR-U filed without full payment is defective.

Other routes

When neither revision nor ITR-U fits

  • Rectification (Section 154): for a mistake apparent from the record in a processed return, such as missed TDS credit.
  • Condonation of delay (Section 119(2)(b)): to claim a refund or carry forward a loss after the time has passed, in genuine hardship.
  • Reply to a notice: if the department has already raised the issue, answer the notice instead of filing ITR-U.

Already got a notice? See Income Tax Notice. Filing this year’s return? See Income Tax e-Filing.

New law

Revised returns, ITR-U and the Income-tax Act, 2025

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to tax year 2026-27 onwards. Corrections for earlier years still follow the 1961 Act.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is the difference between a revised return and an updated return (ITR-U)?

A revised return under Section 139(5) corrects any mistake in a return already filed, including claims that reduce tax, but only within a limited time. An updated return under Section 139(8A), filed as ITR-U, can be filed much later, up to 48 months from the end of the assessment year, but only to report extra income or pay more tax, with additional tax.

Until when can I revise my ITR for FY 2025-26?

A revised return for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) can be filed until 31 March 2027, before the assessment is completed. A fee applies if you revise after 31 December 2026. You can revise more than once within this time.

What is the time limit to file ITR-U?

After the Finance Act, 2025, an updated return can be filed within 48 months from the end of the relevant assessment year, up from 24 months earlier.

How much additional tax is payable on ITR-U?

You pay additional tax on the aggregate of tax and interest due on the updated return: 25% if filed within 12 months from the end of the assessment year, 50% within 24 months, 60% within 36 months and 70% within 48 months.

Can I file ITR-U if I never filed the original return?

Yes. An updated return can be filed even if no original or belated return was filed for that year, as long as it results in tax payable and the other conditions are met.

When can an updated return not be filed?

ITR-U cannot be filed to show a loss, reduce tax, increase a refund, or where a search or survey has begun, an assessment or reassessment is pending or completed for that year, or prosecution proceedings have started. After 36 months, it can be filed only if no reassessment show cause notice was issued, or the officer has decided it is not a fit case.

How many times can I file an updated return?

Only one updated return can be filed for each assessment year. Make sure every missed item is included before filing.

Do I need to pay tax before filing ITR-U?

Yes. Under Section 140B, the tax, interest, late fee and additional tax must be paid before the updated return is filed, and the challan details are reported in the return.

Can I claim a refund through ITR-U?

No. An updated return cannot be used to claim or increase a refund or reduce your tax liability. For mistakes apparent from the record you can seek rectification under Section 154, and in genuine hardship cases, condonation of delay under Section 119(2)(b).

What mistakes can a revised return fix?

A revised return can fix almost anything in the original return: missed income, wrong deductions, wrong ITR form, wrong bank account, or the regime choice where the law allows. It replaces the original return completely.

Does filing ITR-U stop penalties?

Voluntarily disclosing missed income through ITR-U, with the tax and additional tax paid, generally protects you from penalty for under-reporting that income, because you corrected it before the department found it.

Does the Income-tax Act, 2025 change ITR-U?

Revised and updated returns for years up to FY 2025-26 follow the Income-tax Act, 1961. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27 and keeps both facilities with new section numbers, so check the current utility on the e-filing portal.
