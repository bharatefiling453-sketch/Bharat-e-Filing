Overview

What is company compliance?

Company compliance is the set of meetings and filings every private limited company and OPC must complete each year under the Companies Act, 2013 and tax laws: board meetings, the AGM by 30 September, AOC-4 within 30 days and MGT-7 within 60 days of it, plus ITR-6, TDS and GST returns. Late ROC forms cost ₹100 a day each.

It applies from the day the company is incorporated, whether it earns ₹1 or ₹100 crore.

A clean record on the MCA portal matters when you raise funds, take a loan, bid for a tender or sell the business. A company that stops filing can be struck off under Section 248.

Lighter compliance

Small companies and OPCs

  • Small company: paid-up capital up to ₹10 crore and turnover up to ₹100 crore, from 1 December 2025.
  • Excluded: holding and subsidiary companies, Section 8 companies and companies under a special Act.
  • Board meetings: two a year, one in each half, at least 90 days apart.
  • Annual return: abridged MGT-7A; no cash flow statement required.
  • OPC: no AGM; AOC-4 within 180 days of the year end.
Example · Small company

A Jaipur handicrafts private limited with ₹25 lakh capital and ₹6 crore turnover is a small company. It holds two board meetings, its AGM on 28 September, files AOC-4 by 28 October and MGT-7A by 27 November.

Two years without filing (Section 248)

The Registrar can strike off a company that has not filed financial statements or annual returns for two consecutive financial years. Directors of such companies can also be disqualified.

Watch out

What non-compliance costs

  • Late fees: ₹100 a day for each late AOC-4 and MGT-7, with no upper limit.
  • Penalties: on the company and every officer in default under Sections 92 and 137.
  • Director disqualification: for three years if filings are missed for three years (Section 164).
  • Strike-off: after two years without filing (Section 248).
  • DIR-3 KYC: the DIN is deactivated until KYC is filed with a late fee.

Need the tax side too? See Company ITR Filing and TDS Return Filing.

New companies

First-year compliance

  1. First board meeting within 30 days of incorporation.
  2. First auditor appointed by the board within 30 days.
  3. INC-20A (commencement of business) within 180 days, after share money is received.
  4. First AGM within nine months of the end of the first financial year.
New law

Company compliance and the Income-tax Act, 2025

ROC compliance under the Companies Act is unchanged. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to tax year 2026-27, so the company’s tax filings from then follow new references.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is annual compliance for a private limited company?

Annual compliance is the set of filings and meetings every company must complete each year under the Companies Act, 2013 and tax laws: board meetings, the annual general meeting, financial statements in AOC-4, the annual return in MGT-7 or MGT-7A, auditor appointment, director disclosures, and income tax, TDS and GST returns.

What are the due dates for AOC-4 and MGT-7?

The AGM must be held within six months of the end of the financial year, by 30 September. AOC-4 is due within 30 days of the AGM and MGT-7 or MGT-7A within 60 days of the AGM. For an AGM on 30 September 2026, AOC-4 is due by 30 October 2026 and MGT-7 by 29 November 2026.

What is the penalty for late AOC-4 or MGT-7?

Each form filed late attracts an additional fee of ₹100 a day with no upper limit, plus penalties on the company and officers in default under Sections 92 and 137. Not filing for two consecutive years can lead to the company being struck off under Section 248.

How many board meetings must a company hold?

A company must hold its first board meeting within 30 days of incorporation and at least four board meetings a year, with no more than 120 days between two meetings. One person companies, small companies and dormant companies need at least one meeting in each half of the calendar year, at least 90 days apart.

Does a one person company need to hold an AGM?

No. A one person company is exempt from holding an annual general meeting. It must still file its financial statements in AOC-4 within 180 days of the end of the financial year and its annual return in MGT-7A.

What is DIR-3 KYC and how often is it filed?

DIR-3 KYC is the KYC of every person holding a Director Identification Number. From 31 March 2026, it is filed once every three financial years by 30 June, instead of every year. Changes in mobile number, email or address must be updated within 30 days.

What is Form DPT-3?

DPT-3 is an annual return of deposits and of outstanding money received that is not treated as a deposit, such as loans from directors. Every company other than a government company files it by 30 June for the year ended 31 March.

What is Form MSME-1?

MSME-1 is a half-yearly return filed by companies that owe money to micro or small enterprises for more than 45 days. It is due by 30 April for October to March and by 31 October for April to September.

What is a small company and what relief does it get?

From 1 December 2025, a private company with paid-up capital up to ₹10 crore and turnover up to ₹100 crore is a small company, unless it is a holding or subsidiary company, a Section 8 company or governed by a special Act. Small companies hold fewer board meetings, file an abridged annual return and face lower penalties.

When must the first auditor be appointed?

The board must appoint the first auditor within 30 days of incorporation. At the first AGM, the shareholders appoint an auditor for five years, and the company files Form ADT-1 within 15 days of that appointment.

What tax compliances does a company have?

A company files its income tax return in ITR-6, gets a tax audit where required, pays advance tax in four instalments, deducts and deposits TDS and files quarterly TDS returns, and, if registered, files GST returns. For FY 2025-26, ITR-6 is due by 21 November 2026.

Does the Income-tax Act, 2025 change company compliance?

ROC compliance under the Companies Act is unchanged. For tax, the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27, so the company's tax filings for that year onwards follow the new Act's section numbers.
