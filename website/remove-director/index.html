Overview

How do you remove a director from a company?

Shareholders remove a director under Section 169 by an ordinary resolution at a general meeting. Members with at least 1% of the voting power give special notice 14 clear days before the meeting, the director is heard, and the company files DIR-12 within 30 days. The board can’t remove a director on its own.

Removing a director is a shareholders’ right with strict steps. Skip one and the removal can be challenged.

It is governed by Sections 115, 167, 168 and 169 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 23 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014.

Example · Absentee co-founder

Two shareholders of “Kapoor Logistics Private Limited” in Ludhiana, holding 60%, give special notice on 1 November to remove an absentee co-founder. The EGM is held on 20 November with 14 clear days kept, the director’s letter is read out, the resolution passes, and DIR-12 is filed the following week.

Limits

Who can’t be removed this way

  • Tribunal-appointed directors under Section 242.
  • Directors elected by proportional representation, where the articles use Section 163.
  • Not by the board alone: a board resolution can’t remove a director appointed by shareholders.
  • Contract rights stay: removal doesn’t cancel compensation due under an appointment contract.
Good to know

When the office ends on its own

Section 167 vacates the office without a resolution.

  1. 12 months

    Absent from every meeting

    Absent from all board meetings in 12 months, with or without leave.

  2. Section 164

    Disqualified Automatic

    A disqualification vacates the office, with limited exceptions under Section 164(2).

  3. Record it

    DIR-12 still needed 30 days

    The company records the vacation in DIR-12 so the MCA record matches.

The hearing is not optional (Section 169)

The director must get a copy of the special notice and a reasonable opportunity of being heard, including a written representation to members. Skipping this is the most common reason removals are challenged before the Tribunal.

EGM requisition

If the board won’t call the meeting

  1. Requisition by members holding at least one-tenth of the paid-up voting capital (Section 100).
  2. Board calls the EGM within 21 days, for a date within 45 days of the requisition.
  3. If it doesn’t, the requisitionists can call and hold it themselves within three months.
  4. Special notice rules still apply to the removal resolution.

Tax records

Removal and tax liability

Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, directors of a private company can still be held liable for tax dues of the period they held office, if those dues can’t be recovered from the company. Removal doesn’t erase that period.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

How can shareholders remove a director?

Under Section 169 of the Companies Act, shareholders can remove a director before the end of the term by passing an ordinary resolution at a general meeting, after a special notice and after giving the director a reasonable opportunity of being heard. The company then files DIR-12 within 30 days.

What is a special notice for removing a director?

It is a notice of the intention to move the removal resolution, given to the company under Section 115 by members holding at least 1% of the voting power, or shares on which at least ₹5 lakh has been paid up. It must reach the company at least 14 clear days before the meeting, and not earlier than three months before it.

Can the director defend themselves?

Yes. The company sends the director a copy of the special notice, and the director can make a written representation, ask for it to be sent to the members, and speak at the meeting. If it is not sent in time, it can be read out at the meeting.

Can the board remove a director?

No. The board cannot remove a director appointed by the shareholders. Removal under Section 169 needs a resolution of the shareholders at a general meeting. The board can only call that meeting.

What if the board refuses to call the meeting?

Members holding at least one-tenth of the paid-up voting capital can requisition an extraordinary general meeting under Section 100. The board must call it within 21 days, to be held within 45 days; if it does not, the requisitionists can hold it themselves within three months.

Which directors cannot be removed under Section 169?

A director appointed by the Tribunal under Section 242, and directors elected by proportional representation where the articles provide for it under Section 163, cannot be removed this way.

Can a removed director be replaced at the same meeting?

Yes. If special notice of the proposed appointment is also given, the shareholders can appoint another person in the removed director's place at the same meeting. The new director holds office for the remaining term of the removed director.

Does a director's office end automatically in any case?

Yes. Under Section 167, the office is vacated if the director becomes disqualified under Section 164, or is absent from all board meetings held during 12 months, with or without leave, among other grounds. The company still files DIR-12 to record it.

How do I get my name removed as a director of a company?

Resign by giving written notice to the company. The company must file DIR-12 within 30 days, and you can file DIR-11 yourself within 30 days with the reasons. If the company does not file, your DIR-11 gives the Registrar an independent record of the resignation.

Is a removed director entitled to compensation?

Removal does not take away any compensation or damages payable to the director under a contract of appointment, for example as a managing or whole-time director. The terms of the contract decide this.

Is a removed director still liable for the company's past dues?

Removal does not end liability for defaults during the director's tenure. Under the income-tax law, directors of a private company can be held liable for tax dues of the period they were directors if those dues cannot be recovered from the company.

What must be updated after removing a director?

File DIR-12 within 30 days, update the register of directors, apply for the GST core amendment within 15 days, and change the bank's authorised signatories and the income-tax portal contacts.
