Overview

What is an income tax notice?

An income tax notice is a written communication from the Income Tax Department asking you to correct, explain or pay something about your return. It carries a DIN, appears on incometax.gov.in, and names a section and a reply date, usually 15 to 30 days. Most are mismatch notices that a clear reply resolves.

A notice isn’t an accusation. It’s a question, and the right answer, on time, usually ends it.

Notices for years up to FY 2025-26 are issued under the Income-tax Act, 1961. Assessments and appeals are faceless: you reply online under e-Proceedings, and can request a video hearing.

Check the DIN first

Every notice must carry a Document Identification Number and appear on the e-filing portal. Use “Authenticate notice or order” on incometax.gov.in before you reply or pay anything.

Watch out

What happens if a notice is ignored

  • Adjustment made: a 143(1)(a) adjustment goes through and a demand is raised.
  • Return invalid: an unfixed 139(9) defect makes the return as good as not filed.
  • Best judgement: scrutiny can be completed on the department’s own information (Section 144).
  • Penalties: ₹10,000 for non-compliance with a notice; 50% or 200% of tax under Section 270A.
  • Recovery: unpaid demands carry 1% a month interest and can be recovered from bank accounts and refunds.

Avoid notices next year: file with Income Tax e-Filing, or read about the Income-tax Act, 2025 changes.

If the order is wrong

Rectification and appeals

  1. Rectification (Section 154): for mistakes apparent from the record, such as missed TDS credit, within four years.
  2. Appeal to CIT(A): in Form 35 within 30 days of the order.
  3. Appeal to ITAT: against the CIT(A) order, within two months.
  4. Stay of demand: request a stay while the appeal is pending, usually with a part payment.
New law

Notices and the Income-tax Act, 2025

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to tax year 2026-27 onwards. Notices for earlier years still cite the 1961 Act. The kinds of proceedings stay the same; the section numbers change.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

Why did I get an income tax notice?

Most notices are raised automatically when the return does not match the department's data, such as AIS, Form 26AS or TDS returns, when a return is defective or incomplete, when a demand is outstanding, or when a case is picked for scrutiny or reassessment. The section number on the notice tells you what it is about.

How do I check if an income tax notice is genuine?

Every genuine notice carries a Document Identification Number (DIN) and appears on the e-filing portal under Pending Actions, e-Proceedings. You can verify any notice using the Authenticate Notice or Order service on incometax.gov.in.

What is a notice under Section 143(1)(a)?

It is a proposed adjustment to your return, sent before processing, for reasons such as an arithmetical error, an incorrect claim or income shown in Form 26AS or AIS but missing from the return. You can agree or disagree online within 30 days; if you do not respond, the adjustment is made.

What is a defective return notice under Section 139(9)?

It means the return is incomplete or has inconsistencies, such as tax paid but not claimed, or business income without a balance sheet. You must correct the defect within 15 days by filing a corrected return in response, otherwise the return is treated as invalid.

What is a scrutiny notice under Section 143(2)?

It means your return has been selected for detailed assessment. It must be served within three months from the end of the financial year in which the return was filed. Assessment is faceless: you upload replies and documents on the e-filing portal, and can ask for a video hearing.

What is a Section 148A notice?

It is a show cause notice asking why income should not be treated as having escaped assessment, before a reassessment notice under Section 148 is issued. After the 2024 amendment, it can be issued within three years from the end of the assessment year, or five years if escaped income is ₹50 lakh or more. Reply with evidence within the time stated.

What should I do with a demand notice under Section 156?

Check whether the demand is correct. If it is, pay within 30 days to avoid interest of 1% a month under Section 220(2). If it is wrong, submit a response on the portal disagreeing with the demand, file a rectification under Section 154, or appeal.

What is a Section 245 notice?

It is an intimation that the department proposes to adjust your refund against an outstanding demand. You can agree, disagree or partly agree on the portal within 30 days.

What happens if I ignore an income tax notice?

The department can complete the assessment on the information it has, raise a demand with interest, and levy penalties, such as ₹10,000 for not complying with a notice and 50% or 200% of the tax on under-reported or misreported income under Section 270A.

How do I reply to an income tax notice online?

Log in to incometax.gov.in, go to Pending Actions and then e-Proceedings or the Compliance portal, open the notice, and submit your response with supporting documents before the due date. Keep the acknowledgement.

Can I appeal against an assessment order?

Yes. You can appeal to the Commissioner (Appeals) in Form 35 within 30 days of receiving the order, and further to the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal. For mistakes apparent from the record, you can file a rectification request under Section 154.

Does the Income-tax Act, 2025 change income tax notices?

Notices for tax years up to FY 2025-26 continue under the Income-tax Act, 1961 provisions. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27 and renumbers sections, so notices for later years will cite new section numbers for the same kinds of proceedings.
