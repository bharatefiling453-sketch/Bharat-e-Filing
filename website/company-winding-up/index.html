Overview

How do you close a company?

A company that has not carried on business for two financial years, or never started within a year, can apply to the Registrar in STK-2 to strike off its name, after clearing its liabilities, filing pending AOC-4 and MGT-7 and passing a special resolution. A solvent company with assets uses voluntary liquidation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code instead.

An idle company still owes its annual filings, an AGM and an audit every year. Closing it properly ends the cost and the risk of penalties.

Closure is governed by Sections 248 to 252 and 271 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Removal of Names of Companies from the Register of Companies) Rules, 2016 and Section 59 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.

Example · Pivoted startup

“Kaveri Agritech Private Limited” in Mysuru stopped trading in March 2024. It files AOC-4 and MGT-7 for FY 2023-24, cancels GST, closes its bank account, and once FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26 pass without business, files STK-2 in November 2026 with a special resolution and a nil STK-8 statement.

STK-2

Strike off in STK-2, step by step

  1. Stop business and extinguish every liability; satisfy any open charges.
  2. File pending AOC-4 and MGT-7 up to the year business stopped.
  3. Close GST, TAN and the bank account, and file the final ITR-6.
  4. Board meeting and general meeting to pass the special resolution; file MGT-14.
  5. File STK-2 with STK-8, STK-3 and STK-4; C-PACE issues the STK-6 notice, then STK-7.
Good to know

What strike off doesn’t end

Three things directors should know.

  1. Section 248(7)

    Liability for the past

    Directors, officers and members remain liable as if the company still existed.

  2. Section 252

    Restoration Possible

    A member, creditor or workman can ask the Tribunal to restore the name.

  3. Records

    Keep the books Advised

    Keep accounts and closure papers in case of later tax or legal queries.

Don’t just abandon the company

An idle company that isn’t closed keeps owing AOC-4 and MGT-7 every year, each at ₹100 a day when late, and its directors risk disqualification under Section 164(2) after three years of default. Closing early is cheaper.

Liquidation

Voluntary liquidation for solvent companies

  1. Declaration of solvency by a majority of directors, with audited accounts and a valuation report.
  2. Special resolution within four weeks to wind up and appoint an insolvency professional as liquidator.
  3. Creditors’ approval where the company has debts, and notices to the Registrar and the IBBI.
  4. Liquidator sells assets, pays creditors and distributes the rest to members.
  5. Tribunal order dissolving the company, filed with the Registrar.
Tribunal

When the Tribunal winds up a company

  • Special resolution to be wound up by the Tribunal.
  • Acting against the sovereignty, security or interests of India.
  • Fraudulent or unlawful conduct of its affairs.
  • Five consecutive years without filing financial statements or annual returns.
  • Just and equitable grounds.

Keeping the company but pausing it? See Dormant Status Filing.

Tax

Closing tax and GST before STK-2

File the company’s final ITR-6 under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, and pay any tax due. Cancel GST in REG-16 with the final return, and surrender the TAN after the last TDS return.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

How can a company be closed in India?

There are three main routes: voluntary strike off by applying to the Registrar in Form STK-2 under Section 248(2) of the Companies Act, 2013; voluntary liquidation of a solvent company under Section 59 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016; and winding up by the Tribunal under Section 271 of the Companies Act.

Which companies can apply for strike off in STK-2?

A company that has not commenced business within one year of incorporation, or has not carried on any business or operation for the two immediately preceding financial years, can apply after extinguishing all its liabilities, with a special resolution or the consent of members holding 75% of the paid-up share capital.

Which companies cannot apply in STK-2?

Section 249 bars a company from applying if, in the previous three months, it changed its name or registered office state, or disposed of property or rights for value other than in the normal course; or if it is listed, under inspection, investigation or prosecution, holds public deposits, is a Section 8 company, or has a pending scheme of arrangement or winding up.

Do pending AOC-4 and MGT-7 need to be filed before strike off?

Yes. The company must file its overdue financial statements and annual returns up to the end of the financial year in which it stopped business, with the additional fee of ₹100 a day for each late form.

What documents are needed for STK-2?

The special resolution or members' consent, a statement of accounts in STK-8 certified by a chartered accountant and dated not more than 30 days before filing, an indemnity bond in STK-3 and an affidavit in STK-4 from every director, and proof of closure of the bank account and tax registrations.

How long does company strike off take?

Usually four to eight months. Since 1 May 2023, STK-2 applications are processed by the Centre for Processing Accelerated Corporate Exit (C-PACE), which publishes a public notice in STK-6 inviting objections within 30 days before striking off the name.

What is voluntary liquidation of a company?

A solvent company with assets can be wound up under Section 59 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. A majority of directors declare solvency, the members pass a special resolution within four weeks, an insolvency professional is appointed as liquidator to sell the assets and pay creditors, and the Tribunal passes the dissolution order.

When does the Tribunal wind up a company?

Under Section 271 of the Companies Act, the Tribunal can wind up a company if, for example, it passed a special resolution to be wound up by the Tribunal, acted against the sovereignty or security of India, conducted its affairs fraudulently, failed to file financial statements or annual returns for five consecutive years, or if it is just and equitable to do so.

Should GST be cancelled before strike off?

Yes. Cancel the GST registration by applying in REG-16 and filing the final return, surrender the TAN after the last TDS return, and close the bank account before filing STK-2.

Are directors still liable after strike off?

Yes. Under Section 248(7), the liability of every director, officer and member continues and can be enforced as if the company had not been dissolved.

Can a struck-off company be restored?

Yes. A company, member, creditor or workman aggrieved by the strike off can apply to the Tribunal under Section 252. An appeal against the Registrar's order lies within three years, and an application by a member, creditor or workman can be made within twenty years.

Does the company need to file a final income tax return?

Yes. The company files its income tax return in ITR-6 for the final year under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, and settles any tax due before applying for strike off.
