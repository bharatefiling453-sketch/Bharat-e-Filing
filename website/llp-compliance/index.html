Overview

What is LLP compliance?

LLP compliance is the set of filings every limited liability partnership must make each year: Form 11 (annual return) by 30 May, Form 8 (statement of account and solvency) by 30 October, and ITR-5 by the income tax due date. Late MCA forms cost ₹100 a day each, with no upper limit.

It applies from the year the LLP is incorporated, whether it earns ₹1 or ₹10 crore, or nothing at all.

LLP compliance is governed by the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 and the LLP Rules, 2009. Compared with a company, there are no board meetings or AGMs, but the annual forms are just as strict.

Audit

When an LLP needs an audit

  • LLP Act audit: if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh in the year.
  • Tax audit: if turnover exceeds ₹1 crore, or ₹10 crore when cash is within 5% (Section 44AB).
  • Form 8: signed by designated partners and certified by a practising CA, CS or cost accountant.
  • Form 11: certified by a practising company secretary if contribution exceeds ₹50 lakh or turnover exceeds ₹5 crore.
Example · Consultancy LLP

A Hyderabad design consultancy LLP with ₹10 lakh contribution earned ₹60 lakh. Its turnover crosses ₹40 lakh, so its accounts must be audited under the LLP Act, but it is below the tax audit limit. It files ITR-5 by 31 August and Form 8 by 30 October.

Fees never stop on their own

The ₹100 a day additional fee has no upper limit. A form left three years late can cost more than ₹1 lakh in fees alone. Clearing pending years early is always cheaper.

During the year

Partner and other changes

  • Partner joins or leaves: Form 4 within 30 days, with consent where a partner joins.
  • LLP agreement changes: Form 3 within 30 days, for example a new profit ratio or contribution.
  • Registered office moves: Form 15 within 30 days of the change.
  • Contact details of designated partners: DIR-3 KYC update within 30 days.
  • Closing down: Form 24 to strike off an LLP that has stopped business, once filings are up to date.

Filing tax for the LLP? See Partnership Firm & LLP ITR and TDS Return Filing.

Watch out

What non-compliance costs

  1. Late fees: ₹100 a day for each late Form 11 and Form 8.
  2. Penalties: on the LLP and designated partners under Sections 34 and 35.
  3. Strike-off: the Registrar can strike off an LLP not carrying on business.
  4. DIN deactivation: if a designated partner’s KYC lapses.
  5. Income tax: late ITR-5 means a late fee, interest and lost carry-forward of losses.
New law

LLP compliance and the Income-tax Act, 2025

MCA compliance under the LLP Act is unchanged. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to tax year 2026-27, so the LLP’s tax filings from then follow new references.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is annual compliance for an LLP?

Every limited liability partnership must file its annual return in Form 11 and its statement of account and solvency in Form 8 with the Registrar every year, file its income tax return in ITR-5, keep proper books of account, and get an audit where the limits are crossed. Event-based changes, such as new partners, are filed separately.

What are the due dates for LLP Form 11 and Form 8?

Form 11 is due by 30 May, within 60 days of the end of the financial year. Form 8 is due by 30 October, within 30 days of the end of six months from the year end. For FY 2025-26, Form 11 was due by 30 May 2026 and Form 8 is due by 30 October 2026.

What is the late fee for LLP forms?

Each form filed late attracts an additional fee of ₹100 a day, with no upper limit. A Form 8 filed a year late costs over ₹36,000 in additional fees alone, so filing on time matters.

Do LLPs need to file even if there is no business?

Yes. Form 11, Form 8 and the income tax return must be filed every year, even if the LLP had no business or income. An inactive LLP can apply to be struck off, but must first be up to date with its filings.

When does an LLP need an audit?

Under the LLP Act, an LLP's accounts must be audited if its turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or its partners' contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh in the year. Separately, a tax audit under Section 44AB of the Income-tax Act applies when turnover exceeds ₹1 crore, or ₹10 crore if cash transactions are within 5%.

Who signs and certifies Form 8 and Form 11?

Both forms are signed by designated partners with their DSCs. Form 8 is certified by a practising chartered accountant, company secretary or cost accountant. Form 11 must be certified by a practising company secretary when contribution exceeds ₹50 lakh or turnover exceeds ₹5 crore.

What is a small LLP?

A small LLP is one whose partners' contribution does not exceed ₹25 lakh and turnover does not exceed ₹40 lakh, within the limits the LLP Act allows the government to prescribe. Small LLPs face lower penalties for many defaults.

What is the due date for an LLP's income tax return?

An LLP files ITR-5. For FY 2025-26 the due date is 31 August 2026 if no tax audit is required, and 21 November 2026 for tax-audit cases, extended by CBDT Circular 7/2026. An LLP must file with the digital signature of a designated partner.

How do I add or remove a partner in an LLP?

Report the change in Form 4 within 30 days, with the consent of the new partner, and file the change in the LLP agreement in Form 3 within 30 days of the change. Late filing attracts the same ₹100 a day additional fee.

Do designated partners need DIR-3 KYC?

Yes. Designated partners hold a DIN, so they file DIR-3 KYC. From 31 March 2026, it is filed once every three financial years by 30 June, and any change in mobile number, email or address is updated within 30 days.

What happens if an LLP does not file for years?

Fees of ₹100 a day keep running for each form, penalties apply to the LLP and designated partners, and the Registrar can strike off an LLP that is not carrying on business. Designated partners can also face disqualification. Clearing pending years in order is the way out.

Does the Income-tax Act, 2025 change LLP compliance?

MCA compliance under the LLP Act is unchanged. For income tax, the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27, so the LLP's tax filings for that year onwards follow the new section numbers.
