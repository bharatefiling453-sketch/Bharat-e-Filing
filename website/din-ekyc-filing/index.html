Overview

What is DIN eKYC?

DIN eKYC is the KYC every holder of a Director Identification Number files with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs in DIR-3 KYC Web. From 31 March 2026 it is due once every three years by 30 June, and any change in mobile, email or address must be updated within 30 days. Missing it deactivates the DIN.

Your DIN is your identity on every MCA filing. KYC keeps it active, so you can sign, be appointed and stay on record.

It is governed by Section 154 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014, as amended by G.S.R. 943(E) dated 31 December 2025.

Deactivated DIN

If your DIN is deactivated

  • Status: “Deactivated due to non-filing of DIR-3 KYC” on the MCA record.
  • Effect: you can’t be appointed, and filings that need your DIN are blocked.
  • Fix: file DIR-3 KYC Web with the ₹5,000 late fee; the DIN is approved again.
  • Company impact: board forms like DIR-12, AOC-4 and MGT-7 can stall until it’s fixed.

Fewer filings, stricter updates (Rule 12A)

Routine KYC is now once in three years, but a change in your mobile, email or address must be updated within 30 days. Missing either can deactivate your DIN, and reactivation costs a ₹5,000 late fee.

Who files

Who needs to file DIN KYC

  • Every DIN holder: directors of companies and designated partners of LLPs who hold a DIN.
  • Even off a board: the DIN stays allotted to you until it is surrendered.
  • Foreign nationals: file with their passport in place of PAN and Aadhaar.
  • Designated partners of LLPs: see LLP Compliance for Form 8 and Form 11.
Avoid rejections

Common reasons KYC fails

  • Name or date of birth different from the PAN record.
  • PAN not linked with Aadhaar.
  • Shared mobile or email already used by another director.
  • Expired DSC, or a DSC not registered on the MCA portal.
Tax records

DIN KYC and your PAN

DIN KYC checks your details against the Income Tax Department’s PAN database. A valid PAN, linked with Aadhaar, is just as important under the Income-tax Act, 2025, in force from tax year 2026-27, as it was before.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is DIN eKYC?

DIN eKYC is the KYC of a person who holds a Director Identification Number, filed with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs in Form DIR-3 KYC Web. It confirms the director's PAN, Aadhaar, address, personal mobile number and email, verified by OTP and signed with the director's digital signature.

Do directors still need to file DIR-3 KYC every year?

No. The Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Amendment Rules, 2025, effective from 31 March 2026, replaced the annual KYC with a filing once every three financial years, due by 30 June. The earlier 30 September annual deadline no longer applies.

When is my next DIR-3 KYC due?

It depends on when you last filed. For directors whose KYC was up to date in 2025, the next routine filing is generally due by 30 June 2028, though some experts read the rule as 30 June 2029. We confirm your exact window from your DIN record.

What if my mobile number, email or address changes?

Under the amended rules, a change in your personal mobile number, email address or residential address must be updated through DIR-3 KYC Web within 30 days of the change. An update does not reset the three-year cycle.

What happens if DIR-3 KYC is not filed?

The DIN is marked as deactivated due to non-filing of DIR-3 KYC. The person cannot be appointed as a director, and company filings that need that DIN are blocked until the KYC is filed.

How do I reactivate a deactivated DIN?

File DIR-3 KYC Web with the late fee of ₹5,000. Once the form is approved, the DIN status returns to approved and the director can sign and be named in MCA filings again.

Is there a late fee for DIR-3 KYC?

Yes. Filing after the due date, including to reactivate a deactivated DIN, attracts a late fee of ₹5,000.

What documents are needed for DIR-3 KYC?

The director's PAN, Aadhaar, a personal mobile number and email for OTP verification, current residential address, and a valid digital signature certificate. Foreign nationals use their passport in place of PAN and Aadhaar.

Can the same mobile number or email be used by two directors?

No. Each director must give a personal mobile number and email address that are not shared with another director, and both are verified by OTP while filing.

Is DIR-3 KYC needed if I am not a director in any company now?

Yes. KYC applies to every person who holds a DIN, whether or not they are currently on a board, because the DIN remains allotted to them until it is surrendered.

How do I change my name or other DIN particulars?

Changes to particulars such as name, father's name or nationality are made through Form DIR-6 with supporting proof. Changes to mobile number, email or address go through DIR-3 KYC Web within 30 days.

Does DIN KYC need my PAN to match the income-tax records?

Yes. The name and date of birth must match the PAN database of the Income Tax Department, and the PAN should be valid and linked with Aadhaar. Mismatches are a common reason for failed KYC.
