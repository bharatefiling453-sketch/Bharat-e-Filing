Overview

How do you amend a company’s MOA?

To change the objects, the shareholders pass a special resolution and the company files MGT-14 within 30 days. To increase authorised capital, they pass an ordinary resolution if the articles allow it, and the company files SH-7 within 30 days. The change takes effect once the Registrar registers it.

Your memorandum sets the limits of what your company can do. When the business grows past them, the MOA should grow too.

It is governed by Section 13 of the Companies Act, 2013 for most clauses, and Sections 61 and 64 for share capital.

Objects

Changing the objects clause

  • Draft the new objects clearly, covering the activity and what is incidental to it.
  • Special resolution at a general meeting, with an explanatory statement.
  • MGT-14 within 30 days, with the altered MOA attached.
  • Unused public-issue money? Postal ballot, newspaper notice and an exit offer to dissenting shareholders.
  • Name still fits? If not, consider a name change.

Check the articles first (Section 61)

An ordinary resolution can increase authorised capital only if the articles allow it. If they don’t, alter the articles by special resolution in the same meeting, and file MGT-14 as well as SH-7.

Capital

Increasing authorised capital

  1. Check the articles give the power to increase capital.
  2. Board meeting to approve and call a general meeting.
  3. Ordinary resolution altering the capital clause.
  4. SH-7 within 30 days, with the altered MOA.
  5. Then allot shares within the new limit, for example by a rights issue or private placement.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is the memorandum of association?

The memorandum of association, or MOA, is the company's charter. It sets out its name, registered office state, objects, the liability of members, its authorised capital and the subscribers' details. It is altered under Section 13 of the Companies Act, 2013, and under Section 61 for share capital.

How do I change the objects clause of a company?

Hold a board meeting, pass a special resolution of the shareholders approving the altered objects clause, and file it with the Registrar in MGT-14 within 30 days, along with the altered MOA. The change takes effect when the Registrar registers it.

How do I increase the authorised capital of a company?

If the articles allow it, the shareholders pass an ordinary resolution under Section 61 to increase the authorised capital, and the company files SH-7 with the Registrar within 30 days, with the altered capital clause of the MOA. If the articles do not allow it, they are altered first by special resolution.

What is a special resolution?

A special resolution is passed when the votes cast in favour are at least three times the votes cast against, at a general meeting called with notice stating the intention to propose it as a special resolution.

Is MGT-14 needed for an increase in authorised capital?

Only where a special resolution is passed, such as to alter the articles. An increase by ordinary resolution under Section 61 is reported in SH-7.

When does an MOA alteration take effect?

An alteration of the memorandum has effect only when it is registered by the Registrar. The Registrar registers the alteration and certifies it within 30 days of the documents being filed.

Which MOA changes need outside approval?

A change of name needs Registrar approval, a shift of registered office to another state needs Regional Director approval, and a change of objects by a company with unused money raised from the public by prospectus needs extra steps, including a postal ballot, newspaper notice and an exit offer to dissenting shareholders.

Can a company carry on a business not in its objects clause?

It should not. A business outside the objects can be challenged, and banks, licensing authorities and the GST department often check the objects clause. Alter the MOA before starting a new line of business.

Does the company need to adopt a new format MOA?

Companies registered under the old Act often have long objects clauses with main, ancillary and other objects. When altering the objects, many companies adopt a fresh MOA in the format of the Companies Act, 2013 at the same time.

What documents are needed for an MOA amendment?

The board resolution, the notice of the general meeting with the explanatory statement, the special or ordinary resolution, the altered MOA, and a director's digital signature for filing.

Should the company name change when the objects change?

Not necessarily. But if the name describes the old business, for example Textiles or Infra, consider a name change so the name matches the new activity. It is a separate process through RUN and INC-24.

Does an MOA change affect income tax or GST?

The PAN and GSTIN stay the same. Update the nature of business in the GST registration and use the right business code in the income tax return under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27.
