Overview

How do you change your name legally in India?

Swear an affidavit stating your old and new names, publish the change in two newspapers (commonly English and the local language), and apply for publication in the Gazette of India or your State Gazette. Then update Aadhaar and PAN, followed by your bank, passport and other records.

The Gazette notification is the proof most offices accept for a change of name.

Gazette of India publication is handled by the Department of Publication, and applications can be made online. The same spelling must appear in the affidavit, the advertisements and the Gazette.

Sanjeev Kumaar in Patna finds that the school certificates say “Sanjiv Kumar”. Sanjeev swears an affidavit, publishes two ads and applies to the Gazette of India for “Sanjeev Kumar”, then uses the notification to align the PAN, Aadhaar and bank records.

Steps

Name change, step by step

  1. Affidavit: old name, new name and reason, sworn before a notary or oath commissioner.
  2. Newspaper ads: in two newspapers, commonly English and the local language.
  3. Gazette application: with the affidavit, original ads, ID proof and photographs.
  4. Notification: download it once published.
  5. Records: Aadhaar and PAN first, then the rest.

Gazette

Gazette of India or State Gazette?

  • Gazette of India: published by the Department of Publication, with online applications.
  • State Gazette: published by the state government press, where available.
  • Passport Seva: accepts a central or state Gazette notification as proof.
  • Our advice: choose based on where you live and the records you need to update.

Updating your records after the change

  2. PAN: change request with proof of the new name.
  3. Bank and investments: with the updated PAN and Aadhaar.
  4. Passport, voter ID, licence: each with its own application.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

How do I change my name legally in India?

The usual process has three steps: swear an affidavit declaring your old and new names, publish the change in two newspapers, commonly one in English and one in the local language, and apply to have it published in the Gazette of India or your State Gazette. The Gazette notification is the strongest official proof of the change.

Is a Gazette notification compulsory?

Not in every case, but it is the most widely accepted proof. Some agencies accept other documents for certain changes, such as a marriage certificate after marriage, while others ask for the Gazette notification, especially for a complete change of name.

Where is a name change published?

Changes of name by individuals are published in the Gazette of India by the Department of Publication, and applications can be made online. Many states also publish name changes in the State Gazette.

What documents are needed for a name change?

An affidavit stating the old and new names and the reason, the original newspaper advertisements, identity and address proof, recent photographs, and the application in the prescribed format, signed by you and a witness.

How long does the name change take?

The affidavit and newspaper advertisements can usually be done within a few days. Gazette publication takes longer and depends on the publishing office, so plan for several weeks before your new name appears.

Can I change my name after marriage without the Gazette?

Often, yes. A marriage certificate is commonly accepted to change a surname in documents such as the passport and PAN. A Gazette notification is still useful if you want one consistent proof for every record.

How many times can I change my name in Aadhaar?

UIDAI allows the name in Aadhaar to be updated twice in a lifetime, including spelling corrections. Beyond that, a further change needs special approval from UIDAI, supported by documents such as a Gazette notification or court order.

How do I update my PAN after a name change?

Apply for a change in PAN data online and attach proof of the new name, such as the Gazette notification or marriage certificate. Keep the PAN and Aadhaar names consistent, because mismatches can cause problems with linking and tax filings.

Can I change my name in my passport?

Yes. Passport Seva accepts a Gazette notification, central or state, as proof of a change of name, and also lists newspaper clippings with the required documents as an option for some applicants.

Can a minor's name be changed?

Yes. The parent or guardian makes the application and affidavit on behalf of the minor child, with the child's birth certificate and the parent's identity proof.

Which records should I update after changing my name?

Aadhaar and PAN first, then bank accounts, passport, voter ID, driving licence, insurance and investments, property records, and educational and employment records.

Does a name change affect my income tax return?

Your return is filed under your PAN, so update the PAN name and keep it matching Aadhaar. Returns for tax year 2026-27 onwards are filed under the Income-tax Act, 2025; earlier years follow the Income-tax Act, 1961.
