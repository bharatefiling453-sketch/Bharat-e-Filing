Overview

What is Shop Act registration?

Shop Act registration is the registration of a shop, office or commercial establishment under the Shops and Establishments Act of its state. Each state sets its own forms, thresholds and validity. Since the Labour Codes came into force on 21 November 2025, some states accept OSH Code registration in its place.

It’s official proof that your business exists at the premises.

State Acts also govern working hours, weekly holidays and leave. Banks commonly accept the certificate as business proof for a current account.

How to apply

How to apply, step by step

  1. Check your state’s rule for your number of workers.
  2. Gather documents: owner ID, PAN, premises proof, photo.
  3. Apply online on the state labour department portal.
  4. Answer queries from the authority, if any.
  5. Display the certificate or acknowledgement at the premises.

Rules are changing state by state

The Labour Codes have been in force since 21 November 2025, and states are updating their Shops and Establishments rules. What your shop needs depends on your state and number of workers, so we confirm the current position before filing.

Labour Codes

Shop Act and the Labour Codes

  1. 21 November 2025: the Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security and OSH Codes came into force.
  2. OSH Code: provides for registration of establishments with 10 or more workers.
  3. State Acts: continue to apply unless a state changes or repeals them.
  4. Some states: accept OSH Code registration instead of separate Shop Act registration.
Maharashtra

Maharashtra’s 2017 Act

  • 10 or more workers: registration in Form A.
  • Fewer than 10 workers: one-time intimation in Form F.
  • Timing: within 60 days of starting business.
  • April 2026: reportedly, no separate registration if registered under the OSH Code.

Registering as an MSME too? See Udyam Registration.

Tax

Your shop and your taxes

Shop Act registration doesn’t replace tax registrations. Business income is taxed under your PAN: from tax year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025, with FY 2025-26 still under the 1961 Act. GST registration depends on turnover and the type of supply.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is Shop Act registration?

Shop Act registration is the registration of a shop, office or commercial establishment under the Shops and Establishments Act of the state where it operates. Each state has its own Act and rules, which govern registration, working hours, holidays and employment conditions.

Is there one Shop Act for all of India?

No. Shops and establishments are regulated by state laws, so the forms, thresholds, deadlines and validity differ from state to state. A business with premises in several states follows each state's law.

How do the new Labour Codes affect Shop Act registration?

The four Labour Codes came into force on 21 November 2025. The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 provides for registration of establishments with 10 or more workers, while state Shops and Establishments Acts continue to apply unless a state changes them.

Do I still need a separate Shop Act registration?

It depends on your state. Some states have moved to accept registration under the OSH Code in place of separate Shop Act registration; for example, Maharashtra reportedly notified this in April 2026. Others still require registration under their own Shops and Establishments Act. We check your state's current rules before applying.

What did Maharashtra's 2017 Act require?

Under the Maharashtra Shops and Establishments (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 2017, establishments with 10 or more workers registered in Form A, and those with fewer than 10 workers filed a one-time intimation in Form F, within 60 days of starting business.

Who needs Shop Act registration?

Most shops, offices, commercial establishments, restaurants, hotels and similar businesses, including those run from small premises, need to register or file an intimation as their state's law requires. Factories are generally covered by other laws.

What documents are needed?

Typically the owner's identity and address proof and PAN, a photograph, proof of the business premises such as a rent agreement or electricity bill, the number of workers, and entity documents such as a partnership deed, LLP agreement or certificate of incorporation.

Why is the Shop Act certificate useful?

It is official proof that your business exists at the premises. Banks commonly accept it for opening a current account, and it can serve as business proof for other registrations.

Does the registration need renewal?

It depends on the state. Some states issue registrations valid for life, others for a fixed period that must be renewed. We confirm the validity for your state and remind you before any renewal is due.

What if my business details change?

Changes such as a new address, owner, business name or number of workers usually have to be reported to the authority within the time set by your state's rules.

What are the penalties for not registering?

Each state's Act sets its own penalties for running an establishment without the required registration or intimation. Registering on time avoids penalties and inspections.

How does income tax apply to my shop?

Business income from the shop is taxed under your PAN. From tax year 2026-27, returns are filed under the Income-tax Act, 2025; returns for financial year 2025-26 and earlier are filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961.
