Overview

What is professional tax return filing?

Professional tax is a state tax on professions, trades and employments, capped at ₹2,500 a year per person under Article 276 of the Constitution. Employers deduct it from salaries, pay it to the state and file professional tax returns, monthly or annually depending on the state. In Maharashtra, employers file PTRC returns by the 15th.

Small amounts, strict deadlines, and different rules in every state.

Businesses and professionals also pay their own professional tax after enrolling. Late payment attracts interest and late returns attract penalties, so payroll teams keep a calendar for each state.

Example · Two employees in Pune

A Pune firm pays Ravi ₹18,000 and Anita ₹22,000 a month. Ravi’s PT is ₹200 a month and ₹300 in February, ₹2,500 a year. Anita pays nothing, because women in Maharashtra are exempt up to ₹25,000 a month.

How to file

How an employer files a PT return

  1. Frequency: monthly or annual, from last year’s liability.
  2. Work out: apply the state slabs to each employee.
  3. Pay: online on the state tax portal.
  4. File: the return by the due date.
  5. Keep records: challans, returns and salary sheets.

Employers are liable for what they don’t deduct

If an employer fails to deduct or pay professional tax, the state can recover the tax from the employer, with interest and penalties. Each state sets its own rates, so the cost of a missed return differs by state.

Due dates

Maharashtra PTRC due dates

  1. Monthly filers: last year’s liability of ₹50,000 or more.
  2. Monthly due date: the 15th of the following month.
  3. Annual filers: liability below ₹50,000.
  4. Annual due date: 15 March.
  5. Other states: follow their own rules.

Who pays

Who has to pay professional tax

  • Salaried employees: deducted by the employer.
  • Employers: register, deduct, pay and file.
  • Businesses and companies: pay their own PT after enrolling.
  • Professionals: such as doctors, lawyers and CAs, in states that tax them.
Tax

Professional tax and income tax

A salaried person can deduct professional tax from salary income only under the old tax regime. A business deducts the PT it pays as an expense in the year it is actually paid. From tax year 2026-27 these rules are under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is professional tax?

Professional tax is a tax that states levy on professions, trades, callings and employments under Article 276 of the Constitution. The total professional tax payable by one person to a state cannot exceed ₹2,500 a year.

Who pays professional tax?

Salaried employees pay it through their employer, who deducts it from salary and deposits it. Professionals, traders, businesses and companies pay their own professional tax after enrolling with the state.

Do all states levy professional tax?

No. States such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana levy it, each under its own law, rates and due dates. Some states and union territories, such as Delhi, do not levy it.

What are PTRC and PTEC in Maharashtra?

PTRC is the registration certificate an employer needs to deduct and pay professional tax on employees' salaries and file returns. PTEC is the enrolment certificate under which a business, professional or company pays its own professional tax.

What are the Maharashtra salary slabs?

For men, nil up to ₹7,500 a month, ₹175 a month from ₹7,501 to ₹10,000, and ₹200 a month above ₹10,000, with ₹300 in February. For women, nil up to ₹25,000 a month and ₹200 a month above that, with ₹300 in February, so the yearly total is ₹2,500.

What are the Karnataka salary slabs?

From 1 April 2025, nil for monthly pay below ₹25,000, and ₹200 a month for ₹25,000 or more, with ₹300 in February, for a yearly total of ₹2,500.

When are Maharashtra PTRC returns due?

Under the 2026 amendment to the Maharashtra rules, an employer whose tax liability in the previous year was ₹50,000 or more files and pays monthly by the 15th of the following month. Other employers file one annual return by 15 March.

Is professional tax deductible for income tax?

A salaried person can deduct professional tax from salary income only under the old tax regime, not under the new regime. A business can claim the professional tax it pays as an expense in the year it is actually paid. From tax year 2026-27 these rules are under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

What happens if professional tax is paid or filed late?

State laws charge interest for late payment and penalties or late fees for late returns or not registering. An employer who fails to deduct is liable for the tax as well. The amounts differ from state to state.

Are any people exempt?

Each state lists its own exemptions. In Maharashtra, for example, they include people over 65 and people with a disability of 40% or more, subject to the conditions in the law.

What does an employer have to do?

Register in each state where it has employees, deduct professional tax from salaries at the state's slab, deposit it by the due date, file returns, and keep salary and payment records for inspection.

What if a business has employees in more than one state?

Professional tax follows the state where the employee works. The employer registers in each such state that levies the tax and follows that state's slabs, returns and due dates.
