Overview

What is a One Person Company?

A One Person Company (OPC) is a company with a single member, defined in section 2(62) of the Companies Act, 2013. It’s a separate legal entity with limited liability, formed by an Indian citizen, resident or not, who names a nominee to take over if needed. It’s incorporated online through SPICe+.

The control of a proprietorship, with the protection of a company.

Since 1 April 2021, NRIs can form an OPC, the residency test is 120 days, and the old capital and turnover limits are gone, so an OPC can grow and convert into a private company whenever it chooses.

How to register

How to register an OPC, step by step

  1. DSC: a Class 3 digital signature for the director.
  2. Name: ending with “(OPC) Private Limited”, reserved through SPICe+.
  3. SPICe+: with e-MoA, e-AoA, nominee consent and office proof.
  4. Certificate of incorporation: with the CIN, PAN and TAN.
  5. Start up: bank account, first auditor in 30 days, INC-20A in 180 days.

A company, with company duties

An OPC enjoys limited liability, but it’s still a company: its accounts are audited every year, it files AOC-4 and MGT-7A with the MCA, and it pays tax as a company, not as an individual.

Compliance

Yearly compliance for an OPC

  1. Audit: accounts audited by a chartered accountant every year.
  2. AOC-4: financial statements within 180 days of the year end (section 137).
  3. MGT-7A: abridged annual return, usually due by late November.
  4. DIR-3 KYC: director KYC as the rules require.
  5. Income tax: company return every year, with TDS as applicable.

Compare

OPC or proprietorship?

  • Liability: limited in an OPC; unlimited in a proprietorship.
  • Succession: through the nominee in an OPC.
  • Compliance: audit and MCA filings for an OPC; far fewer for a proprietorship.
  • Tax: company rates for an OPC; individual rates for a proprietor.
Tax

How an OPC is taxed

An OPC is taxed as a domestic company. It can choose a concessional rate of 22%, plus surcharge and cess, by giving up certain deductions. From tax year 2026-27, this is under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is a One Person Company?

A One Person Company (OPC) is a company with only one person as its member, defined in section 2(62) of the Companies Act, 2013. It is a separate legal entity with limited liability, so the owner's personal assets are generally protected from the company's debts.

Who can form an OPC?

Only a natural person who is an Indian citizen, whether resident in India or not, can incorporate an OPC or be its nominee. Since 1 April 2021, Indian citizens who are not resident in India, such as NRIs, can also form one.

What does resident in India mean for an OPC?

For OPC rules, a person resident in India is one who has stayed in India for at least 120 days during the immediately preceding financial year. This replaced the earlier 182-day test from 1 April 2021.

Can one person form more than one OPC?

No. A natural person can incorporate only one OPC, or be a nominee in only one OPC, at a time.

Why does an OPC need a nominee?

The sole member must name a nominee who will become the member if the member dies or becomes incapable of contracting. The nominee's written consent is filed with the incorporation application.

Is there a limit on capital or turnover for an OPC?

Not any more. The Companies (Incorporation) Second Amendment Rules, 2021 removed the earlier paid-up capital and turnover limits that forced an OPC to convert, and allow an OPC to convert voluntarily into a private or public company at any time.

How many directors can an OPC have?

At least one and up to fifteen directors. The sole member can also be the sole director.

How is an OPC incorporated?

Online on the MCA portal through the SPICe+ form, with the electronic memorandum and articles of association. The application also covers the company's PAN and TAN, and can include GST, EPFO and ESIC registrations and a bank account through linked forms.

What compliances does an OPC have after incorporation?

Filing INC-20A within 180 days of incorporation, appointing the first auditor within 30 days, filing financial statements in AOC-4 within 180 days of the end of the financial year, filing the abridged annual return in MGT-7A, and filing an income tax return every year.

Does an OPC hold an annual general meeting?

No. Section 96 of the Companies Act exempts an OPC from holding an annual general meeting.

How is an OPC taxed?

An OPC is taxed as a domestic company, not as an individual. It can choose a concessional company tax rate of 22% plus surcharge and cess by giving up certain deductions. From tax year 2026-27 this is under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

What is the difference between an OPC and a proprietorship?

A proprietorship is not separate from its owner, so the owner's liability is unlimited. An OPC is a separate company with limited liability and perpetual succession through its nominee, but it has more compliance, including audited accounts and MCA filings.
