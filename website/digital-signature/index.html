Overview

What is a digital signature certificate?

A digital signature certificate (DSC) is an electronic certificate issued by a licensed Certifying Authority under the Information Technology Act, 2000. It proves your identity online and lets you sign MCA forms, income tax returns, GST returns and e-tenders with the legal effect of a handwritten signature. Class 3 is the class issued now.

It’s stored on a PIN-protected USB token, and only you should hold both.

Certifying Authorities are licensed and supervised by the Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA), which sets the identity verification rules every applicant goes through.

Your DSC is your signature

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000, a digital signature has the legal effect of your handwritten signature. Never hand your token and PIN to anyone else; anything signed with it is treated as signed by you.

Where used

Where you need a DSC

  • MCA: company and LLP e-forms, DIR-3 KYC, incorporation.
  • Income tax: company returns; professionals’ audit reports.
  • GST: company registration applications and returns.
  • E-tenders: government procurement portals, usually a combo DSC.

Classes

Why only Class 3 now

The CCA directed Certifying Authorities to stop issuing Class 2 certificates from 1 January 2021. Class 3 certificates use stronger identity verification and are accepted across government portals.

Income tax

DSC and the Income-tax Act, 2025

The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27; returns for financial year 2025-26 are still filed under the 1961 Act. On the e-filing portal, companies verify returns with a DSC, while most individuals can use Aadhaar OTP or EVC.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is a digital signature certificate (DSC)?

A DSC is an electronic certificate issued by a licensed Certifying Authority under the Information Technology Act, 2000. It proves your identity online and lets you sign electronic documents and forms, such as MCA filings, income tax returns and GST returns, with the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.

Which class of DSC should I buy?

Class 3. The Controller of Certifying Authorities directed Certifying Authorities to stop issuing Class 2 certificates from 1 January 2021, so Class 3 is the class issued now for individuals and organisations.

Who issues a DSC in India?

Only Certifying Authorities licensed by the Controller of Certifying Authorities under the Information Technology Act, 2000 can issue a DSC. We apply on your behalf through a licensed Certifying Authority.

How is my identity verified?

Through one of the methods allowed by the Controller of Certifying Authorities, such as Aadhaar eKYC with OTP and video verification, Aadhaar biometric eKYC, or document-based verification. The Certifying Authority confirms your mobile number and email during the process.

Do I need a DSC for MCA filings?

Yes. Company and LLP e-forms on the MCA portal are signed with the DSC of an authorised director, designated partner or professional, and directors also use it for DIR-3 KYC. The DSC must be registered on the MCA portal before use.

Do I need a DSC to file my income tax return?

Companies must verify their income tax return with a DSC. Most other taxpayers can e-verify with Aadhaar OTP or an electronic verification code, though a registered DSC can also be used.

Do I need a DSC for GST?

Companies must sign their GST registration application and returns with a DSC under Rule 26 of the CGST Rules, 2017. Other registered persons can usually use an electronic verification code instead.

What are signature, encryption and combo DSCs?

A signature DSC is used to sign documents and filings. An encryption DSC is used to encrypt documents, mainly for e-tendering. A combo DSC has both, and is often needed for government e-tenders.

How long is a DSC valid?

A DSC is valid for the period shown on the certificate, usually one or two years from issue. After that it must be renewed by applying for a fresh certificate.

What is a USB token?

The DSC is stored on a secure USB crypto token protected by a PIN. You plug in the token to sign. Keep it safe and never share the PIN, because anyone with both can sign as you.

Can I use one DSC on several portals?

Yes. The same personal DSC can be registered and used on the MCA, income tax and GST portals, as long as the name and PAN details match each portal's records.

How does the new Income-tax Act affect DSC use?

The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27. Returns for financial year 2025-26 and earlier are still filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961. Companies continue to sign their returns with a DSC on the e-filing portal.
