Overview

How do you change a company’s registered office?

Within the same city, a board resolution and INC-22 within 30 days are enough. Moving to another city in the state needs a special resolution filed in MGT-14. A move to another Registrar’s area or another state also needs Regional Director approval in INC-23. The company, its PAN and contracts stay the same.

The registered office is where the law, the Registrar and the tax department reach your company. Keep it current and nothing gets missed.

It is governed by Sections 12 and 13 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rules 25, 27, 28 and 30 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014. LLPs follow Section 13 of the LLP Act, 2008 and Rule 17 of the LLP Rules.

Example · Same state move

“Verma Agro Foods Private Limited” moves its registered office from Nashik to Pune, both under the ROC Pune. The board meets on 1 November, shareholders pass a special resolution at an EGM on short notice on 5 November, and MGT-14 and INC-22 are filed the following week.

State to state

Shifting the registered office to another state

  1. Board meeting to approve the shift and call a general meeting.
  2. Special resolution to alter the memorandum; file MGT-14 within 30 days.
  3. Newspaper notice in INC-26, in English and in the local language, within 30 days before the application.
  4. Notices to creditors, debenture holders, the Registrar, the State Government and SEBI if listed.
  5. INC-23 application to the Regional Director, with the creditors list and declarations.
  6. RD order, then INC-28 within 30 days, and INC-22 for the new address.

A shift isn’t allowed while an inquiry, inspection or investigation is under way, or a prosecution under the Act is pending (Rule 30).

Don’t miss the 30-day window (Section 12)

Notice of every change of registered office must reach the Registrar in INC-22 within 30 days. Late filing attracts additional fees, and a penalty of ₹1,000 a day, up to ₹1 lakh, for the company and each officer in default.

LLP

Changing the registered office of an LLP

  1. Partners’ consent as required by the LLP agreement.
  2. Another state? Publish a newspaper notice and get the consent of secured creditors first.
  3. Form 15 to the Registrar within 30 days, with address proof and the owner’s NOC.
  4. Form 3 if the LLP agreement is changed.

Running an LLP? See LLP Compliance for Form 8 and Form 11.

After the move

What to update after the move

  • GST: core amendment of the principal place of business within 15 days. See GST Amendment.
  • New state: fresh GST registration there, and cancel or keep the old one as a branch.
  • PAN and TAN: update the address so notices reach you.
  • Bank, Udyam, IEC, shop and trade licences: update each record.
  • Signboard and stationery: name and registered office address outside the office, and on letterheads and invoices (Section 12).
Tax records

Office move and your tax records

A move doesn’t create a new taxpayer. The company keeps its PAN and TAN, and its returns continue under the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27. Update the address so the right assessing officer and every notice reach you.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

How do I change the registered office of a company within the same city?

If the new address is within the local limits of the same city, town or village, the board passes a resolution approving the new address and the company files INC-22 with the Registrar within 30 days of the change, with proof of the new office.

What if the registered office moves to another city in the same state?

A move outside the local limits of the city needs a special resolution of the shareholders, filed in MGT-14 within 30 days, and INC-22 within 30 days of the change. If the new place falls under a different Registrar of Companies in the same state, the change must also be confirmed by the Regional Director on an application in INC-23.

How do I shift a registered office from one state to another?

Pass a special resolution to alter the memorandum, advertise the proposal in Form INC-26 in an English and a vernacular newspaper, notify creditors, debenture holders and the State Government, and apply to the Regional Director in INC-23. After the order, file it with the Registrar in INC-28 within 30 days, and file INC-22 for the new address.

How long does a state-to-state shift take?

It usually takes two to three months. The newspaper notice, objections from creditors or others, and the Regional Director's review set the pace. Under Section 13(4), the Central Government is to decide the application within 60 days.

Which form is filed for a change of registered office?

INC-22 is filed for every change of registered office address, within 30 days of the change. MGT-14 is filed for the special resolution where one is needed, INC-23 for Regional Director approval, and INC-28 to file the Regional Director's order.

What documents are needed for INC-22?

Proof of the new address, such as a registered title document or a notarised rent agreement with a recent rent receipt, a no-objection certificate from the owner, and a utility bill not older than two months, along with the board or special resolution.

What is the penalty for not filing INC-22 on time?

Under Section 12(8) of the Companies Act, the company and every officer in default can face a penalty of ₹1,000 for each day the default continues, up to ₹1 lakh, in addition to additional fees for late filing.

When can a company not shift its registered office to another state?

Under Rule 30, the shift is not allowed if an inquiry, inspection or investigation has been started against the company, or a prosecution under the Companies Act is pending against it. Objections from creditors or employees must also be settled.

How does an LLP change its registered office?

With the consent of the partners as required by the LLP agreement, the LLP files Form 15 with the Registrar within 30 days. For a move to another state, the LLP first publishes a notice in newspapers and obtains the consent of its secured creditors. Form 3 is filed if the LLP agreement changes.

Does a registered office change affect GST?

Within the same state, the GSTIN stays the same and the principal place of business is updated through a core amendment within 15 days of the change. GST registration is state-wise, so moving to another state needs a new GST registration in that state.

Does the PAN or CIN change when the registered office moves?

The PAN stays the same; only the address on record is updated. The CIN includes a state code, so after a state-to-state shift the company gets a new CIN reflecting the new state, while remaining the same company.

Must the new address be displayed?

Yes. Under Section 12, the company must paint or affix its name and registered office address outside every office, and print the registered office address, CIN, phone, email and website on its letterheads, invoices and official documents.
