Overview

What is Udyam Registration?

Udyam Registration is the official registration of micro, small and medium enterprises with the Ministry of MSME. It’s done online with the Aadhaar of the proprietor, partner or director and the enterprise’s PAN, as a self-declaration with no documents to upload, and gives a permanent Udyam Registration Number.

It’s the MSME identity that banks, buyers and government schemes recognise.

Classification follows the MSMED Act, 2006 and the limits notified in S.O. 1364(E) dated 21 March 2025, in force from 1 April 2025. Investment and turnover figures are linked to your income tax and GST records.

Cross either limit and you move up a category; you move down only when you fall below both. Export turnover is excluded.

Example · Export-heavy unit

A garment unit in Tiruppur has ₹1.5 crore in machinery and ₹14 crore turnover, of which ₹5 crore is exports. Its turnover for classification is ₹9 crore, within both micro limits, so it registers on Udyam as a micro enterprise.

How to register

How to register on Udyam, step by step

  1. Aadhaar OTP: of the proprietor, managing partner, karta or authorised signatory.
  2. PAN validation: income tax and GST details are linked through it.
  3. Business details: name, address, activity, NIC codes, bank and employees.
  4. Declare investment and turnover, and submit with OTP.
  5. Certificate: receive the Udyam number and download the certificate.

Your right to be paid on time

Buyers must pay a micro or small enterprise within the agreed period, no more than 45 days, or 15 days without a written agreement. Late payment carries compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate, and the buyer can’t claim the tax deduction until paid.

Late payments

When a buyer pays late

  1. Due date: agreed period up to 45 days, or 15 days without agreement (Section 15).
  2. Interest: compound, monthly rests, at three times the RBI bank rate (Section 16).
  3. Reminder: a written demand citing the MSMED Act.
  4. Council: apply to the MSE Facilitation Council through MSME Samadhaan.

These protections cover micro and small enterprises, not medium ones.

For companies

MSME Form 1

  • Who: companies owing micro or small enterprises for over 45 days.
  • When: half-yearly, by 30 April and 31 October.
  • What: amounts due and reasons for the delay.

Running a company too? See Company Compliance.

Tax

The income tax rule for buyers

Under section 43B(h) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, carried into section 37(2)(g) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27, a buyer can deduct an amount owed to a micro or small enterprise only in the year it is actually paid, if it isn’t paid within the MSMED Act period.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is Udyam Registration?

Udyam Registration is the official registration of micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) with the Ministry of MSME. It is done online on the Udyam Registration portal using Aadhaar, and gives the enterprise a permanent Udyam Registration Number and certificate.

What are the MSME limits from 1 April 2025?

Under notification S.O. 1364(E) dated 21 March 2025, a micro enterprise has investment in plant and machinery or equipment up to ₹2.5 crore and turnover up to ₹10 crore; a small enterprise up to ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore; and a medium enterprise up to ₹125 crore and ₹500 crore.

How is the category decided if one limit is crossed?

Both investment and turnover are considered. If an enterprise crosses either limit for its category, it moves to the next higher category, but it moves down only when it falls below both limits of its current category.

Is export turnover counted?

No. Turnover from exports is excluded when calculating turnover for MSME classification.

What do I need to register?

The Aadhaar number of the proprietor, managing partner, karta or authorised signatory, the PAN of the enterprise, and GSTIN where the business is required to be registered under GST. Investment and turnover details are linked to income tax and GST records.

Do I need to upload documents?

No. Udyam Registration is based on self-declaration, and no documents or certificates need to be uploaded. The details are checked against government databases such as PAN and GST.

Do I need to renew Udyam Registration?

No. The registration is permanent, but the details should be kept updated. Investment and turnover are updated using income tax and GST data, and the category can change each year.

Can traders register on Udyam?

Yes. Retail and wholesale traders can register on Udyam, but their registration is for priority sector lending benefits only.

Within how many days must buyers pay MSE suppliers?

Under Section 15 of the MSMED Act, 2006, a buyer must pay a micro or small enterprise within the period agreed in writing, which cannot exceed 45 days from acceptance, or within 15 days if there is no written agreement.

What if a buyer pays a micro or small enterprise late?

The buyer must pay compound interest with monthly rests at three times the bank rate notified by the RBI under Section 16 of the MSMED Act. The supplier can also apply to the MSE Facilitation Council through the MSME Samadhaan portal.

How does late payment affect the buyer's income tax?

Under section 43B(h) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and section 37(2)(g) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27, a buyer can deduct an amount owed to a micro or small enterprise only in the year it is actually paid, if it is not paid within the time allowed under the MSMED Act.

What is MSME Form 1?

Companies that owe micro or small enterprises for more than 45 days must file MSME Form 1 with the MCA every half-year, disclosing the amounts and reasons for delay: by 30 April for October to March, and by 31 October for April to September.
