Basics

What is GST registration?

GST registration is the process by which a supplier of goods or services is enrolled under the Goods and Services Tax law and allotted a unique 15-digit Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN). It is governed by Sections 22 to 30 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 and Rules 8 to 26 of the CGST Rules, 2017. Registration is PAN-based and state-specific: a business needs a separate GSTIN in every state or union territory from which it makes taxable supplies.

Once registered you can legally collect GST from customers, claim input tax credit (ITC) on GST paid on purchases, issue tax invoices and sell across state borders and on e-commerce marketplaces.

How to read a GSTIN

Eligibility

Who needs GST registration? Turnover limits for 2026

Under Section 22, registration becomes mandatory within 30 days of your aggregate turnover (all-India, same PAN, including exempt and inter-state supplies but excluding GST) crossing the threshold in a financial year.

GST registration threshold limits (aggregate turnover per financial year)
Type of supplier Most states & UTs Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura
Exclusively goods ₹40 lakh* ₹20 lakh
Services, or goods + services ₹20 lakh ₹10 lakh
Composition scheme (optional ceiling) ₹1.5 crore (goods) / ₹50 lakh (services) ₹75 lakh (goods)

*The ₹40 lakh goods limit (Notification 10/2019-CT) is not available in every state — a few states and UTs retained ₹20 lakh. We confirm the limit for your state before filing.

Compulsory registration — even with ₹0 turnover (Section 24)

  • Inter-state taxable supply of goods
  • Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons
  • Persons liable to pay tax under reverse charge (RCM)
  • E-commerce operators and persons required to deduct TDS / collect TCS under GST
  • Suppliers of goods through e-commerce operators — see relief below
  • Input Service Distributors (ISD) and agents supplying on behalf of others
  • Suppliers of online information & database access (OIDAR) services from outside India
Relief for small online sellers

Since 1 October 2023, suppliers of goods below the threshold can sell within their own state on marketplaces without registration, subject to notified conditions (PAN, declared state, enrolment with the portal). Service providers below the threshold selling through e-commerce operators are also exempt.

Example

Priya runs a home bakery in Pune. Her sales for FY 2026-27 reach ₹38 lakh by February, all within Maharashtra. Bakery products are goods, so the ₹40 lakh limit applies and she need not register yet. But in March she receives a ₹5 lakh corporate order from Bengaluru — inter-state supply of goods triggers compulsory registration under Section 24 before she ships, regardless of turnover.

Example

Arjun is a freelance UI designer in Jaipur billing ₹1.8 lakh a month (₹21.6 lakh a year) to Indian clients. Services attract the ₹20 lakh limit, so he must register within 30 days of crossing ₹20 lakh. Exporting services to a US client counts towards aggregate turnover too — and with a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) he can export without paying IGST.

Statutory timelines (Rule 9, CGST Rules)

Officer must approve or raise a query within 7 working days if Aadhaar is authenticated. Without Aadhaar authentication, or if the application is risk-flagged, physical verification applies and the window extends up to 30 days. If the officer takes no action in time, registration is deemed approved.

Risk

Penalty for not registering under GST

  • Penalty (Section 122): ₹10,000 or the tax evaded, whichever is higher; 100% of tax due where fraud is involved.
  • Interest (Section 50): 18% p.a. on tax not paid from the date it was due.
  • No input tax credit for the unregistered period, and buyers cannot claim ITC on your invoices.
  • Prosecution (Section 132) for serious evasion above notified monetary limits.
Worked example

A trader in Indore crossed ₹40 lakh in October but registered only in March, missing GST of ₹1,20,000 on sales in between. Exposure: tax ₹1,20,000 + interest at 18% (≈ ₹9,000 for an average 5-month delay) + penalty of at least ₹12,000 (10% of tax, minimum ₹10,000, in non-fraud cases under Section 122(2)). Registering on time would have cost ₹0 in government fees.

GST × Income tax

GST registration and the Income-tax Act, 2025

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 from 1 April 2026. GST rules are unchanged, but the income-tax provisions every GST-registered business deals with now have new section numbers. The income-tax department matches GST turnover with the turnover in your ITR, so both must reconcile.

Old vs new income-tax references relevant to GST-registered businesses
Topic Income-tax Act, 1961 Income-tax Act, 2025
Period of income Previous year & assessment year Single “tax year”
Presumptive taxation (business, profession, transport) Sections 44AD, 44ADA, 44AE Section 58
Tax audit Section 44AB Section 63
TDS on contractors, professional fees, e-commerce, purchase of goods Sections 194C, 194J, 194-O, 194Q Section 393 (consolidated tables)
Transition note

Income of FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) is still assessed under the 1961 Act. The 2025 Act applies from tax year 2026-27 onwards. Under presumptive taxation, business income is deemed at 8% of turnover (6% for digital receipts) under both Acts — and your GST returns are the turnover evidence.

FAQs

GST registration — frequently asked questions

What is the turnover limit for GST registration in 2026?

Registration is mandatory once aggregate turnover in a financial year crosses ₹40 lakh for suppliers of goods or ₹20 lakh for suppliers of services. In special category states (Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura) the limits are ₹20 lakh for goods and ₹10 lakh for services; a few other states have also kept ₹20 lakh for goods.

Is there any government fee for GST registration?

No. The GST portal charges no government fee for new registration. You pay only the professional fee of your consultant if you choose assisted filing.

How long does GST registration take?

With successful Aadhaar authentication the officer must act within 7 working days. Eligible small B2B suppliers opting for Rule 14A (from 1 November 2025) get registration electronically within 3 working days. If Aadhaar authentication is not done or the application is risk-flagged, physical verification can extend it up to 30 days.

What is Rule 14A GST registration?

Rule 14A of the CGST Rules, inserted by Notification 18/2025-Central Tax with effect from 1 November 2025, is an optional fast-track for applicants whose output tax liability on supplies to registered persons will not exceed ₹2.5 lakh per month. After Aadhaar authentication, registration is granted electronically within 3 working days. Only one such registration per PAN per state is allowed.

Can I get GST registration without a shop or office?

Yes. You can register using your residential address as the principal place of business, supported by an electricity bill or property tax receipt and, if the property is not in your name, a consent letter or rent agreement from the owner.

Is GST registration mandatory for selling on Amazon or Flipkart?

Generally yes under Section 24 of the CGST Act. However, since 1 October 2023 small suppliers of goods below the threshold may sell intra-state through e-commerce operators without registration, subject to notified conditions, and service providers below the threshold selling through e-commerce operators are exempt.

Is a bank account required at the time of GST registration?

Under Rule 10A you can furnish bank account details within 30 days of grant of registration or before filing GSTR-1, whichever is earlier. Many applicants add it in the application itself to avoid a missed deadline.

What is the penalty for not taking GST registration?

Under Section 122 of the CGST Act, the penalty is ₹10,000 or the tax evaded (100% in cases of fraud), whichever is higher, plus 18% interest under Section 50 on the unpaid tax.

Can I register for GST voluntarily below the threshold?

Yes. Section 25(3) allows voluntary registration. It lets you claim input tax credit, issue tax invoices to B2B customers and sell inter-state or online, but you must then file all GST returns.

How is the 15-digit GSTIN structured?

Digits 1–2 are the state code, 3–12 are the PAN, 13 is the entity number for that PAN in the state, 14 is 'Z' by default and 15 is a check digit.

Does the Income-tax Act, 2025 change anything for GST-registered businesses?

GST law is unaffected, but from 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act. 'Previous year' and 'assessment year' become 'tax year', presumptive taxation under sections 44AD/44ADA/44AE moves to section 58, tax audit under section 44AB moves to section 63, and most TDS sections (194C, 194J, 194-O, 194Q) are consolidated in section 393. Your GST turnover should still reconcile with the turnover in your income-tax return.

Which returns do I file after GST registration?

Regular taxpayers file GSTR-1 (outward supplies) and GSTR-3B (summary and tax payment) monthly or quarterly under QRMP, and GSTR-9 annually if applicable. Composition taxpayers file CMP-08 quarterly and GSTR-4 annually.
