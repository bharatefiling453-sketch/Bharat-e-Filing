Overview

What is dormant company status?

Dormant status under Section 455 lets a company that is inactive for two years, or kept for a future project or to hold an asset, stay registered with lighter compliance. It applies in MSC-1 after a special resolution, files MSC-3 every year, and can return to active status through MSC-4.

Not every pause needs a closure. Dormant status keeps the company ready for later, at a fraction of the yearly effort.

It is governed by Section 455 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rules 3 to 8 of the Companies (Miscellaneous) Rules, 2014.

Example · Future project

“Reddy Infra Holdings Private Limited” in Vijayawada holds land for a project planned in three years and has had no business since April 2024. It passes a special resolution, files MSC-1 in July 2026, receives MSC-2, and files MSC-3 every April after that.

Blockers

When a company can’t apply

  • Inspection, inquiry or investigation ordered or pending, or a prosecution pending.
  • Public deposits outstanding, or a default on them.
  • Statutory, tax or workmen’s dues outstanding.
  • Dispute over management or ownership, or listed securities.
  • Unsecured loan? Allowed, with the lender’s concurrence.
Good to know

What a significant transaction is

These payments don’t break dormancy.

  1. Allowed

    Registrar and legal fees

    Payments to the Registrar and to meet legal requirements.

  2. Allowed

    Office and records Upkeep

    Paying to maintain the office and records, and share allotments the law requires.

  3. Not allowed

    Business activity Ends dormancy

    Sales, purchases or other trading mean MSC-4 within seven days.

Dormant is not forever (Section 455(6))

The Registrar can strike off a company that stays dormant for five consecutive financial years. Plan to reactivate or close before then. If the company resumes business earlier, directors must apply in MSC-4 within seven days.

Yearly upkeep

What a dormant company still does

  • MSC-3 within 30 days of each financial year end, that is by 30 April.
  • Board meetings: one in each half of the calendar year, at least 90 days apart.
  • Minimum directors: two for a private company, three for a public company, one for an OPC.
  • Active DINs for every director. See DIN eKYC Filing.
Reactivation

Becoming active again

  1. Decide to restart, or note the act that ended dormancy.
  2. File MSC-4 with an MSC-3 return for the period, within seven days if business has resumed.
  3. Receive MSC-5, the Registrar’s certificate of active status.
  4. Resume full compliance: AOC-4, MGT-7 and ADT-1. See Company Compliance.
Tax records

Dormant company and income tax

Dormant status is under the Companies Act only. The company keeps its PAN and still files ITR-6 every year under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, even with no income.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is a dormant company?

Under Section 455 of the Companies Act, 2013, a dormant company is one formed for a future project or to hold an asset or intellectual property with no significant accounting transaction, or an inactive company, that has obtained dormant status from the Registrar of Companies.

What is an inactive company?

An inactive company is one that has not carried on any business or operation, or has made no significant accounting transaction, during the last two financial years, or has not filed its financial statements and annual returns during the last two financial years.

What is a significant accounting transaction?

Any transaction other than payments to the Registrar, payments to fulfil the requirements of the Act or other laws, allotment of shares to fulfil legal requirements, and payments for maintaining the company's office and records.

How does a company apply for dormant status?

It passes a special resolution, or obtains the consent of at least three-fourths of its shareholders in value, and files Form MSC-1 with the Registrar along with the supporting documents. The Registrar issues a certificate of dormant status in Form MSC-2.

Which companies cannot apply for dormant status?

A company cannot apply if an inspection, inquiry or investigation has been ordered or is pending, if a prosecution is pending against it, if it has public deposits outstanding or is in default on them, if it has outstanding statutory dues or workmen's dues, if there is a dispute in management or ownership, or if its securities are listed. A company with an unsecured loan can apply with the lender's concurrence.

What must a dormant company file every year?

A dormant company files a return of dormant company in Form MSC-3 within 30 days from the end of each financial year, that is by 30 April, along with the documents the rules require, and keeps the minimum number of directors.

How many board meetings must a dormant company hold?

Under Section 173(5), a dormant company must hold at least one board meeting in each half of a calendar year, with a gap of at least 90 days between the two meetings.

How long can a company stay dormant?

The Registrar can strike off the name of a company that remains dormant for five consecutive financial years. Before that, the company should decide whether to become active again or to close.

How does a dormant company become active again?

It files Form MSC-4 with a return in Form MSC-3 for the relevant period, and the Registrar issues a certificate of active status in Form MSC-5. If the company does anything inconsistent with its dormant status, its directors must apply within seven days.

What are the benefits of dormant status?

A dormant company has lighter compliance than an active company: fewer board meetings, a short annual return in MSC-3, and no cash flow statement, while keeping its name, CIN and assets intact for future use.

Is dormant status better than closing the company?

Dormant status suits a company that may be used later, for a future project or to hold an asset. If there is no plan to use it, closing it through strike-off may cost less over time. We advise on the better route for your case.

Does a dormant company still file income tax returns?

Yes. A dormant company keeps its PAN and must file its income tax return in ITR-6 every year under the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27, even if it has no income.
