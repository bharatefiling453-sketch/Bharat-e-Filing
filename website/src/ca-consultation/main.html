Overview

What is a CA consultation?

A CA consultation is a one-to-one session with a practising chartered accountant, by phone or video, on income tax, capital gains, GST, business set-up, company compliance or a tax notice. You get advice on your own facts, the numbers worked out, and a written summary of what to do next.

The right advice before you act is cheaper than fixing a mistake later.

Our CAs are members of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, bound by its Code of Ethics, including confidentiality. Ask for the CA’s membership number and verify it on the ICAI website.

Advice, not a guess

Tax rules changed with the Income-tax Act, 2025 from 1 April 2026. Forum answers and old articles may quote sections that no longer apply. A CA checks your facts against the current law before advising.

Income-tax Act, 2025

What the new Income-tax Act changes

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 from 1 April 2026. The first year under the new law is tax year 2026-27.

Old Act vs. new Act
Point 1961 Act 2025 Act
Year conceptPrevious year + assessment yearTax year
Applies toUp to FY 2025-26From tax year 2026-27
Section numbersOld numberingRenumbered

Returns and notices for financial year 2025-26 and earlier still follow the 1961 Act.

Advance tax

Advance tax instalments for tax year 2026-27

  • 15 June 2026: at least 15% of the year’s tax.
  • 15 September 2026: at least 45%.
  • 15 December 2026: at least 75%.
  • 15 March 2027: 100%, or the full amount for presumptive taxpayers.
