Overview

What are revised and updated returns?

A revised return (Section 139(5)) corrects any mistake in an ITR you filed, until 31 March 2027 for FY 2025-26. An updated return (ITR-U) (Section 139(8A)) lets you report missed income up to 48 months after the assessment year ends, by paying tax, interest and 25% to 70% additional tax.

Both let you set the record straight yourself, which always costs less than the department finding it first.

A revised return replaces the original completely. An updated return adds to it, and can be filed only once for each assessment year.

Example · Missed share sale

Sneha, a teacher in Nagpur, forgot a short-term gain on shares in her AY 2025-26 return. The tax and interest come to ₹30,000. Filing ITR-U before 31 March 2027 adds 25%, ₹7,500. Waiting past that date would double it to ₹15,000.

Example · Missed deduction

Arjun, an engineer in Gurugram, forgot his parents’ health insurance under Section 80D in his FY 2025-26 return. He files a revised return before 31 December 2026 and gets a higher refund. ITR-U couldn’t have done this.

Watch out

When an updated return can’t be filed

  • It would show a loss, reduce tax or increase a refund
  • A search, survey or requisition has begun for that year
  • An assessment or reassessment is pending or completed for that year
  • Prosecution has started, or the department has information under certain laws
  • After 36 months, if a reassessment show cause notice was issued (unless the officer found it not a fit case)

Pay before you file (Section 140B)

An updated return is valid only if the tax, interest, late fee and additional tax are paid before it is filed. An ITR-U filed without full payment is defective.

Other routes

When neither revision nor ITR-U fits

  • Rectification (Section 154): for a mistake apparent from the record in a processed return, such as missed TDS credit.
  • Condonation of delay (Section 119(2)(b)): to claim a refund or carry forward a loss after the time has passed, in genuine hardship.
  • Reply to a notice: if the department has already raised the issue, answer the notice instead of filing ITR-U.

Already got a notice? See Income Tax Notice. Filing this year’s return? See Income Tax e-Filing.

New law

Revised returns, ITR-U and the Income-tax Act, 2025

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to tax year 2026-27 onwards. Corrections for earlier years still follow the 1961 Act.
