Overview

How do you reactivate a deactivated DIN?

A DIN deactivated for missed KYC is reactivated by filing DIR-3 KYC Web with a flat ₹5,000 late fee, using your PAN, Aadhaar, a personal mobile and email verified by OTP, and your DSC. Once approved, the status returns to approved. A DIN disqualified under Section 164(2) needs a different route.

A deactivated DIN blocks your signature on MCA filings. The fix is usually one form, if your details match.

KYC is governed by Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014, as amended from 31 March 2026. Disqualification is under Section 164(2) of the Companies Act, 2013.

Mr Rao, a director of “Rao Engineering Private Limited” in Hyderabad, finds his DIN deactivated when the company’s AOC-4 won’t accept his signature. He files DIR-3 KYC Web with the ₹5,000 late fee, his DIN is approved again, and AOC-4 goes through.

Disqualified

If your DIN is disqualified under Section 164(2)

  • Cause: a company where you are a director didn’t file financial statements or annual returns for three continuous years, or defaulted on deposits, debentures or dividends for a year.
  • Effect: you can’t be appointed or reappointed in any company for five years, and your office in other companies is vacated (Section 167).
  • Not a KYC issue: filing DIR-3 KYC won’t lift it.
  • Options: wait out the period, revive a struck-off company through the Tribunal (Section 252), or correct a wrong marking.

Check before you pay the late fee

The ₹5,000 KYC filing fixes a DIN deactivated for missed KYC. It doesn’t lift a Section 164(2) disqualification, and a surrendered DIN isn’t reactivated. We confirm your status first, so you pay only for the right fix.

While deactivated

What a deactivated DIN blocks

  • New appointments: you can’t be appointed as a director or designated partner.
  • Your signature: MCA forms that need your DIN, such as DIR-12, AOC-4 and MGT-7, can’t be filed with it.
  • Company deadlines: late company filings can attract additional fees of their own. See Company Compliance.
Avoid rejections

Common reasons reactivation fails

  • Name or date of birth different from the PAN record.
  • PAN not linked with Aadhaar.
  • Shared mobile or email already used by another director.
  • Expired DSC, or a DSC not registered on the MCA portal.
Tax records

Reactivation and your PAN

The reactivation KYC checks your details against the Income Tax Department’s PAN database. Keep your PAN valid and linked with Aadhaar, as required under the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27.
