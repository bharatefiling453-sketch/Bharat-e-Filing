Overview

What is LLP Form 11 and when is it due?

Form 11 is the annual return of a limited liability partnership. It reports the LLP’s partners, designated partners and their contribution, and is filed with the Registrar by 30 May, within 60 days of the year end. Every LLP files it, even with no business. Late filing costs ₹100 a day.

Form 11 tells the Registrar who runs your LLP and what they put in. File it on time and it costs nothing extra.

It is governed by Section 35 of the LLP Act, 2008 and Rules 24 and 25 of the LLP Rules, 2009.

Certification

Who signs and certifies Form 11

  • Designated partner signs with a valid DSC and an active DIN or DPIN.
  • Practising company secretary certifies it where contribution exceeds ₹50 lakh or turnover exceeds ₹5 crore.
  • Body corporate partners act through their nominees, whose details are given.
Good to know

The LLP filing year

Two ROC returns and one tax return, every year.

  1. 30 May

    Form 11, annual return

    Partners and contribution as at the year end.

  2. Tax due date

    ITR-5 Income tax

    The LLP’s income tax return, by the due date for its case.

  3. 30 October

    Form 8 Accounts

    Statement of account and solvency, certified by a professional.

₹100 a day, with no upper limit (Rule 24)

A Form 11 filed a year late carries over ₹36,000 in additional fees, for one form. Three missed years can pass ₹1 lakh. File the pending years now to stop the count.

Late filing

If Form 11 is already late

  1. List every pending year from the LLP’s MCA record.
  2. Rebuild partner history, filing any missing Form 4 first.
  3. File each Form 11 in order, each with its own late fee.
  4. Follow with Form 8 for the same years.

Planning to close the LLP? Pending returns usually have to be filed first.

Partners

Keep the partner record current

  • Form 4 within 30 days of a partner joining or leaving.
  • Form 3 within 30 days of a change in the LLP agreement.
  • At least two designated partners, one resident in India.
  • Active DIN for every designated partner. See DIN eKYC Filing.
Tax records

Form 11 and the LLP’s tax return

Form 11 goes to the Registrar; the LLP’s income tax return is ITR-5 under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27. Partner and contribution figures should match across the two.
