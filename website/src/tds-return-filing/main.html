Overview

What is TDS return filing?

A TDS return is a quarterly statement of tax deducted at source, filed by every deductor with a TAN. It lists each payee’s PAN, amount paid, TDS deducted and the challan used to pay it. TDS returns are due by 31 July, 31 October, 31 January and 31 May, and a late return costs ₹200 a day.

The return is what turns your TDS deposits into credit in your payees’ Form 26AS and AIS. Miss it, and both you and they feel it.

Returns are filed under Section 200(3) and the TDS rules, through the e-filing portal, and processed on TRACES, where you download certificates and see any defaults.

Example · Late return

A Jaipur interior firm deducted ₹80,000 TDS in July to September but filed its 26Q on 20 November, 20 days late. The late fee is ₹200 × 20 = ₹4,000. Had it waited past a year, a penalty of up to ₹1 lakh could also apply.

What’s new

Latest changes in TDS

Updates that matter, newest first.

  1. 1 April 2026

    Income-tax Act, 2025 in force

    TDS and TCS for tax year 2026-27 follow the new Act, with provisions consolidated under new section numbers. Check current forms before filing.

  2. 1 April 2025

    Higher thresholds Law change

    Professional fees ₹50,000, commission ₹20,000, rent ₹50,000 a month, non-bank interest ₹10,000. TDS on partners (194T) started.

  3. 1 April 2025

    Section 206AB removed Act now

    The higher TDS rate for non-filers is gone. Section 206AA still applies when a payee gives no PAN.

File within a year to avoid the penalty (Section 271H)

No penalty applies if the TDS, interest and late fee are paid and the return is filed within one year of the due date. After that, the penalty can be ₹10,000 to ₹1 lakh.

TRACES defaults

Common TRACES defaults and how they’re fixed

  • Short payment: the challan doesn’t cover the TDS reported. Pay the balance and tag the challan in a correction.
  • Short deduction: a lower rate was applied. Pay the difference with interest.
  • Late payment interest: TDS deposited after the 7th. Pay 1.5% a month or part.
  • Late filing fee: ₹200 a day under Section 234E, if not paid with the return.
  • Invalid PAN: correct the PAN, or deduct at the Section 206AA rate.

New law

TDS returns and the Income-tax Act, 2025

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 governs TDS and TCS for tax year 2026-27. Quarterly returns continue; returns for earlier quarters and corrections still follow the 1961 Act.
