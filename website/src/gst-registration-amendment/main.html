Overview

What is GST registration amendment?

GST registration amendment updates the details on your GST registration, such as address, business name, partners, contact details or bank account, through Form GST REG-14. File it within 15 days of the change. Core changes need the officer’s approval within 15 working days; non-core changes apply at once.

Your GST registration is a public record. Buyers, suppliers and officers rely on it, so it has to match where and how you actually do business.

Amendments are governed by Section 28 of the CGST Act, 2017 and Rule 19 of the CGST Rules. The officer approves core changes in Form REG-15, may raise a query in REG-03, and can reject an application in REG-05. If the officer does not act within the time limit, the change is treated as approved.

Example · Address change

Kavya’s bakery in Hyderabad moves to a bigger shop in the same city on 15 October 2026. She must file REG-14 by 30 October with the new rent agreement and the owner’s electricity bill. The officer has 15 working days to approve it; once approved, her updated certificate shows the new address and her GSTIN stays the same.

Example · New partner

A Surat textile firm admits a third partner. Adding a partner is a core change, so the firm files REG-14 with the amended partnership deed and the partner’s PAN, Aadhaar and photograph. Because the firm’s PAN does not change, no new GSTIN is needed.

What’s new

Latest changes that affect amendments

Updates that matter when you change your GST details, newest first.

  1. 1 October 2026

    Multistate Registration New

    Starting business in another state still needs a separate GSTIN for that state, but you can now apply for several states together under one Master TRN.

  2. 1 April 2026

    Income-tax Act, 2025 in force

    GST registration is linked to your PAN. Keep your name and address consistent on the income-tax and GST portals.

  3. November 2025

    Bank account on every registration

    GSTN enforced Rule 10A: a registration without bank details can be suspended. Adding or changing a bank account is a quick non-core amendment.

  4. 22 September 2025

    GST 2.0 rates

    With new 5% and 18% slabs, review the goods and services (HSN/SAC) listed on your registration and update them as a non-core amendment if needed.

Deemed approval (Rule 19, CGST Rules)

If the officer neither approves nor raises a query within 15 working days of your core amendment application, the change is treated as approved and your registration is amended.

Watch out

When an amendment is not enough

  • New PAN: a proprietorship becoming a partnership, or a firm becoming an LLP or company, needs a fresh registration.
  • New state: moving to or opening in another state needs a GSTIN in that state.
  • Composition scheme: opting in or out is done through CMP forms, not REG-14.
  • Cancelled registration: restore it first through revocation, then amend.

Not updating on time can attract a general penalty of up to ₹25,000 each under the CGST and SGST Acts (Section 125), and an outdated address can lead to suspension if the officer cannot verify it.

After approval

What to do after your amendment is approved

  1. Download the amended certificate (REG-06) and display it at your new premises.
  2. Update invoices and e-way bills with the new name or address.
  3. Inform customers and suppliers so their records match yours.
  4. Update e-invoicing and accounting software with the new details.
  5. Keep the proof of the change with your GST records.
Income tax

GST amendment and the Income-tax Act, 2025

GST registration is PAN-based. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act, so keep your name, address and business details consistent across both portals.
