Overview

What is ICEGATE registration?

ICEGATE, the Indian Customs Electronic Gateway, is the customs portal of the CBIC. Registering lets importers, exporters and customs brokers track bills of entry and shipping bills, pay duty online, upload documents through e-Sanchit, and track IGST refunds and drawback. You need an active IEC and, for DSC-based registration, a Class 3 DSC.

For exporters, ICEGATE is also where the bank account for refunds is registered.

Email and mobile details should match those held by DGFT and, where relevant, GSTN, and the name should match PAN records.

Example · First-time exporter

Saffron Weaves in Panipat plans its first export of rugs in December 2026, paying IGST. It registers on ICEGATE with its IEC and a Class 3 DSC, registers its bank account and AD code, and reports the export invoice in GSTR-1 so the shipping bill can be processed as the refund claim.

How to register

How to register on ICEGATE, step by step

  1. Check the IEC is active and updated, and contact details match DGFT.
  2. Arrange a Class 3 DSC for the authorised signatory.
  3. Apply on ICEGATE: choose the user type, enter IEC details and verify by OTP.
  4. Sign and submit the application with the DSC.
  5. After approval, register the bank account and AD code for refunds.

Refunds need the whole chain

An IGST refund on exports moves only when the shipping bill, EGM, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B match, and the bank account is registered with Customs and validated by PFMS. A sanctioned refund may not be credited to an unvalidated or closed account.

IGST refund

How the IGST export refund works

  1. Shipping bill filed for goods exported on payment of IGST.
  2. Export manifest (EGM) filed by the shipping line or airline.
  3. GSTR-1 reports the export invoice; GSTR-3B is filed.
  4. Shipping bill treated as the refund claim under Rule 96 of the CGST Rules.
  5. Refund credited to the bank account registered with Customs, through PFMS.
Bank and AD code

Bank account and AD code registration

  • Bank account linked to the IEC, registered on ICEGATE.
  • PFMS validation of the account before refunds can be credited.
  • AD code of the bank registered with Customs at the ports of export.
  • Same details in Customs and GST records avoid mismatches.

Exporting without paying IGST instead? See GST LUT Filing.

Tax

Exports and your other tax filings

Export income is taxed as business income. From tax year 2026-27, returns are filed under the Income-tax Act, 2025; returns for FY 2025-26 still follow the 1961 Act. GST returns report exports separately.
