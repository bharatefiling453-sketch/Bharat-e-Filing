Overview

What is a legal consultation?

A legal consultation is a one-to-one session with an advocate enrolled with a State Bar Council, by phone or video. You explain your matter, and the advocate tells you where you stand, your options and the deadlines that apply, followed by a written note of the advice.

Many legal rights come with short deadlines. Advice early is often the difference.

Bharat e-Filing arranges the consultation; the advice comes from independent advocates bound by the Bar Council of India Rules on professional conduct. We are not a law firm.

Cheque bounce

The Section 138 timeline

  1. Cheque returned unpaid; the bank issues a return memo.
  2. Demand notice in writing within 30 days of receiving the memo.
  3. 15 days for the drawer to pay after receiving the notice.
  4. Complaint within one month after those 15 days end.

Missing a step can weaken the case, so act as soon as the memo arrives.

Consumer

Where a consumer complaint goes

  • District Commission: amount paid up to ₹50 lakh.
  • State Commission: above ₹50 lakh, up to ₹2 crore.
  • National Commission: above ₹2 crore.

Complaints can be filed online on the e-Daakhil portal, generally within two years of the cause of action.
