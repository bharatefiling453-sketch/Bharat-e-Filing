Overview

What is INC-20A?

INC-20A is the declaration for commencement of business under Section 10A of the Companies Act, 2013. Every company with a share capital incorporated on or after 2 November 2018 must file it within 180 days of incorporation, confirming that each subscriber has paid for the shares agreed in the memorandum.

Until it’s filed, the company can’t commence business or exercise its borrowing powers.

The form is prescribed by Rule 23A of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014. A director signs it, and a practising chartered accountant, company secretary or cost accountant certifies it.

“Nilgiri Brew Labs Private Limited” in Coimbatore was incorporated on 10 August 2026 with two subscribers of ₹50,000 each. Both pay into the company’s current account in September, and it files INC-20A with the bank statement well before the 6 February 2027 deadline.

How to file

How to file INC-20A, step by step

  1. Open the company’s bank account with the certificate of incorporation and PAN.
  2. Collect subscription money from every subscriber, as agreed in the memorandum.
  3. Download the bank statement showing each payment.
  4. Director signs INC-20A with a DSC; a practising professional certifies it.
  5. File on the MCA portal within 180 days and keep the SRN.

Don’t start trading first

A company with share capital can’t commence business or borrow until INC-20A is filed. Missing the 180 days means a ₹50,000 penalty on the company, up to ₹1,00,000 per officer, and the Registrar may move to strike off the name.

Penalty

Section 10A(2) penalties
Who Penalty
Company₹50,000
Each officer in default₹1,000 a day, up to ₹1,00,000

Late filing also attracts higher additional fees that rise with the delay.

Strike-off risk

When the Registrar can act

  • No INC-20A within 180 days of incorporation.
  • Reasonable cause to believe the company isn’t carrying on business.
  • Strike-off action under Section 248, after notice to the company.

Tax

Tax set-up once business starts

The company files its first return in ITR-6 under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, even for a short first year. PAN and TAN come with SPICe+; register for GST where turnover or the business requires it.
