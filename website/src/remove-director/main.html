Overview

How do you remove a director from a company?

Shareholders remove a director under Section 169 by an ordinary resolution at a general meeting. Members with at least 1% of the voting power give special notice 14 clear days before the meeting, the director is heard, and the company files DIR-12 within 30 days. The board can’t remove a director on its own.

Removing a director is a shareholders’ right with strict steps. Skip one and the removal can be challenged.

It is governed by Sections 115, 167, 168 and 169 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 23 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014.

Example · Absentee co-founder

Two shareholders of “Kapoor Logistics Private Limited” in Ludhiana, holding 60%, give special notice on 1 November to remove an absentee co-founder. The EGM is held on 20 November with 14 clear days kept, the director’s letter is read out, the resolution passes, and DIR-12 is filed the following week.

Limits

Who can’t be removed this way

  • Tribunal-appointed directors under Section 242.
  • Directors elected by proportional representation, where the articles use Section 163.
  • Not by the board alone: a board resolution can’t remove a director appointed by shareholders.
  • Contract rights stay: removal doesn’t cancel compensation due under an appointment contract.
Good to know

When the office ends on its own

Section 167 vacates the office without a resolution.

  1. 12 months

    Absent from every meeting

    Absent from all board meetings in 12 months, with or without leave.

  2. Section 164

    Disqualified Automatic

    A disqualification vacates the office, with limited exceptions under Section 164(2).

  3. Record it

    DIR-12 still needed 30 days

    The company records the vacation in DIR-12 so the MCA record matches.

The hearing is not optional (Section 169)

The director must get a copy of the special notice and a reasonable opportunity of being heard, including a written representation to members. Skipping this is the most common reason removals are challenged before the Tribunal.

EGM requisition

If the board won’t call the meeting

  1. Requisition by members holding at least one-tenth of the paid-up voting capital (Section 100).
  2. Board calls the EGM within 21 days, for a date within 45 days of the requisition.
  3. If it doesn’t, the requisitionists can call and hold it themselves within three months.
  4. Special notice rules still apply to the removal resolution.

  • File DIR-11 yourself within 30 days, with the reasons.

  • Past liability for your tenure continues, so keep your records.

Tax records

Removal and tax liability

Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from tax year 2026-27, directors of a private company can still be held liable for tax dues of the period they held office, if those dues can’t be recovered from the company. Removal doesn’t erase that period.
