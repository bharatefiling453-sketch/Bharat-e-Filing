Overview

How does a company name change work?

To change a company’s name, reserve the new name through RUN, pass a special resolution, file it in MGT-14 within 30 days and apply to the Registrar in INC-24. The Registrar issues a fresh certificate of incorporation. The company, its PAN, GSTIN and contracts stay the same; only the name changes.

A name change is a rebrand on paper and in law. Done right, customers, banks and the tax department all follow without friction.

It is governed by Section 13 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 29 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014. LLPs follow Section 13 of the LLP Act, 2008.

Name rules

What a new name can and can’t be

  • Not identical or too similar to an existing company, LLP or registered trademark.
  • Correct ending: “Private Limited”, “(OPC) Private Limited”, “Limited” or “LLP”.
  • Restricted words need approval: Bank, Insurance, Stock Exchange, Mutual Fund, Board, Commission, Authority, National, Union, Central, Federal, Republic.
  • Not undesirable: offensive words or names that suggest a link with the government.
  • No filing defaults: annual returns and financial statements must be up to date (Rule 29).

File everything pending first (Rule 29)

The Registrar won’t approve a name change while annual returns or financial statements are pending, or deposits are in default. We check your MCA record before reserving the name, so you don’t lose the 60-day window.

LLP

Changing the name of an LLP

  1. Reserve the name through RUN-LLP.
  2. Partners’ consent as required by the LLP agreement.
  3. Form 5 for the change of name, within 30 days of the consent.
  4. Form 3 for the change in the LLP agreement, within 30 days.
  5. Fresh certificate of incorporation in the new name.

Running an LLP? See LLP Compliance for Form 8 and Form 11.

After approval

What to update after the name change

  • PAN and TAN: change request with the new certificate; numbers stay the same.
  • GST: core amendment for the legal name within 15 days. See GST Amendment.
  • Bank accounts: letter with the new certificate and board resolution.
  • Udyam, IEC, shop and trade licences: update each registration.
  • Stationery and website: show the new name with “formerly known as” for two years (Section 12).
Tax records

Name change and your tax records

A name change doesn’t create a new taxpayer. The company keeps its PAN, TAN and GSTIN, and its returns continue as before, under the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27.
