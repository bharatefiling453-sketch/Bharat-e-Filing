Overview

What is an Import Export Code?

An Import Export Code (IEC) is a 10-character code issued by DGFT. Under Section 7 of the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992, no one can import or export for trade without one, unless exempt. It is PAN-based, has no expiry, and must be updated every year between April and June.

It’s the first registration every importer and exporter needs.

You apply online on the DGFT portal and sign with a digital signature certificate or Aadhaar e-sign. Customs, banks and the GST system all use the IEC to identify you as a trader.

Moradabad Brassworks, a proprietorship, receives its first order from Dubai in November 2026. The owner applies for an IEC on the DGFT portal with their PAN, shop address proof and a cancelled cheque, signs with Aadhaar e-sign, and registers on ICEGATE before the shipment.

How to apply

How to apply for an IEC, step by step

  1. Register on the DGFT portal with the applicant’s email and mobile.
  2. Fill the application: PAN, entity, address, bank and partners or directors.
  3. Upload address proof and the cancelled cheque or bank certificate.
  4. Sign and submit with a DSC or Aadhaar e-sign.
  5. Download the IEC and diarise the annual update.

An IEC can lapse without the update

The IEC never expires, but it must be updated every year between 1 April and 30 June, even if nothing has changed. An IEC that isn’t updated can be deactivated, holding up customs clearance and refunds until it’s updated.

Annual update

The annual IEC update

  1. Window: 1 April to 30 June, every year.
  2. Who: every IEC holder, even with no changes.
  3. How: confirm or update details online on DGFT.
  4. Missed it: the IEC can be deactivated; updating it allows reactivation.
Changes

Changing details in your IEC

  • Address of the business or branches.
  • Bank account linked to the IEC.
  • Partners or directors joining or leaving.
  • Contact details used for OTPs and notices.

Tax

IEC, GST and income tax

The IEC is a trade registration, not a tax registration. GST returns report imports and exports, and export income is taxed as business income: from tax year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025, with FY 2025-26 still under the 1961 Act.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is an Import Export Code (IEC)?

An IEC is a 10-character code issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT). Under Section 7 of the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992, no person can import or export except under an IEC, unless specifically exempted.

Who needs an IEC?

Any business or individual importing or exporting goods for trade, including proprietors, partnerships, LLPs and companies. Exporters of services generally need an IEC to claim benefits under the Foreign Trade Policy.

Is the IEC linked to PAN?

Yes. The IEC is based on the applicant's PAN, and one PAN can hold only one IEC. The business name and details in the application should match the PAN records.

What documents are needed for an IEC?

PAN of the business or individual, proof of the business address, and bank proof such as a cancelled cheque with the applicant's name pre-printed or a bank certificate. Companies, LLPs and firms also provide details of their directors or partners.

How is the IEC application signed?

The online application on the DGFT portal is signed with a digital signature certificate or with Aadhaar-based e-sign. For Aadhaar e-sign, the Aadhaar should be linked to the applicant's PAN and mobile number.

How long does it take to get an IEC?

Once the application is complete and signed, the IEC is usually generated online quickly, often the same day. We submit your application within 24 hours of receiving complete details.

Does an IEC expire?

An IEC has no expiry date, but it must be updated or confirmed on the DGFT portal every year between 1 April and 30 June, even if nothing has changed.

What happens if I miss the annual IEC update?

An IEC that is not updated in the April to June window is deactivated. It can be reactivated by completing the update on the DGFT portal, but shipments and benefits linked to it can be held up until then.

Can I change details in my IEC?

Yes. Changes such as address, bank account, partners or directors are made online through an IEC modification on the DGFT portal.

Is an IEC needed for imports for personal use?

Goods imported or exported for personal use that are not connected with trade, manufacture or agriculture are exempt from the IEC requirement. Some government bodies and notified cases are also exempt.

What do I need after getting the IEC?

Exporters usually register on ICEGATE, register their bank account and AD code with Customs, and furnish a GST Letter of Undertaking if they want to export without paying IGST.

How is income from exports taxed?

Export income is taxed as business income. From tax year 2026-27, income tax returns are filed under the Income-tax Act, 2025; returns for financial year 2025-26 and earlier are filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961.
