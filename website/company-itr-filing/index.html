Overview

What is company ITR filing?

Every company files its income tax return in ITR-6 every year, even with a loss, signed with a digital signature. It pays 22% under Section 115BAA or 25% or 30% under the normal regime, plus surcharge and cess. For FY 2025-26, ITR-6 is due by 21 November 2026.

The company return sets this year’s tax, protects losses and MAT credit for later years, and is read by banks, investors and acquirers.

Returns for FY 2025-26 are filed under Section 139(1) of the Income-tax Act, 1961. The company also files its accounts and annual return with the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act, 2013.

Example · Agency

A Noida digital agency has ₹50 lakh of income and ₹5 lakh of deductions it would lose under 115BAA. Normal tax on ₹45 lakh at 25% plus cess is about ₹11.7 lakh; 115BAA on ₹50 lakh is about ₹12.6 lakh. Here the normal regime is cheaper, unless MAT on book profit pushes it higher.

MAT & losses

MAT, losses and startup benefits

  1. MAT (Section 115JB): under the normal regime, pay at least 15% of book profit plus surcharge and cess. MAT credit carries forward for 15 years.
  2. Losses: business losses carry forward for 8 years, only if ITR-6 is filed by the due date.
  3. Shareholding change (Section 79): a closely held company loses its losses if more than 49% of shareholding changes, with relief for eligible startups.
  4. Startup holiday (Section 80-IAC): DPIIT startups incorporated before 1 April 2030 can deduct 100% of profits for 3 years out of 10.
The 115BAA choice is permanent

Once you file Form 10-IC and opt for Section 115BAA, you can’t go back to the normal regime. Unused MAT credit and some deductions are lost, so model at least three years before switching.

ROC filings

Annual ROC filings for a company

  • AGM: within six months of the year end, by 30 September.
  • AOC-4 (financial statements): within 30 days of the AGM.
  • MGT-7 or MGT-7A (annual return): within 60 days of the AGM; MGT-7A for small companies and OPCs.
  • ADT-1 (auditor appointment): within 15 days of the AGM where an auditor is appointed.
  • Late fee: ₹100 a day for each late AOC-4 or MGT-7, with no upper limit.

File on time to keep your losses (Section 80)

Business losses carry forward for 8 years, but only if ITR-6 for the loss year is filed by the due date. Companies must file even in a loss year.

Watch out

If your company misses a deadline

  • Late ITR fee: up to ₹5,000 under Section 234F.
  • Interest: 1% a month or part on unpaid tax from the due date (Section 234A).
  • Losses: business losses of the year can’t be carried forward from a late return.
  • Prosecution: wilful failure to file can be prosecuted under Section 276CC.
  • ROC forms: ₹100 a day for each late AOC-4 or MGT-7, with no cap.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

Which ITR form does a company file?

Every company files ITR-6, except a company claiming exemption under Section 11 for income from property held for charitable or religious purposes, which files ITR-7. ITR-6 must be filed with the digital signature (DSC) of a director or authorised signatory.

Is it mandatory for a company to file an income tax return?

Yes. Every company must file its return every year under Section 139(1), whatever its income, even if it made a loss or had no business during the year.

What is the due date for company ITR filing for FY 2025-26?

Companies are audited under the Companies Act, so they file by the audit-case due date. For FY 2025-26 it is 21 November 2026, extended from 31 October by CBDT Circular 7/2026. Companies with international or specified domestic transactions that need a transfer pricing report file by 30 November 2026.

What is the income tax rate for a company?

A domestic company can opt for Section 115BAA and pay 22% plus a 10% surcharge and 4% cess, about 25.17%, without most exemptions and without MAT. Otherwise it pays 25% if its turnover in FY 2023-24 did not exceed ₹400 crore, or 30%, plus a surcharge of 7% above ₹1 crore and 12% above ₹10 crore, and 4% cess.

Should my company opt for Section 115BAA?

It usually saves tax when the company has few deductions or incentives to claim. Companies claiming large deductions, such as Section 80-IAC for startups, or holding unused MAT credit may pay less under the normal regime. Once chosen, the 115BAA option cannot be withdrawn, so compare both before deciding.

How do I opt for Section 115BAA?

File Form 10-IC on the income tax portal on or before the due date for filing the return for the first year you choose the option, then file ITR-6 under the new rate. A late Form 10-IC can lead to the option being denied.

What is MAT and when does it apply?

Minimum Alternate Tax under Section 115JB applies to companies under the normal regime when tax on book profit at 15%, plus surcharge and cess, is higher than tax on normal income. MAT paid can be carried forward as a credit for 15 years. Companies under Section 115BAA do not pay MAT.

Can a company carry forward its losses?

Yes. Business losses can be carried forward for eight years if the return is filed by the due date. For a closely held company, losses lapse if more than 49% of the shareholding changes, except for eligible startups where all original shareholders continue.

What is the tax holiday for startups?

An eligible startup recognised by DPIIT and incorporated before 1 April 2030 can claim a 100% deduction of profits under Section 80-IAC for any three consecutive years out of its first ten, after obtaining the Inter-Ministerial Board certificate.

What ROC filings does a company have every year?

After the annual general meeting, which must be held by 30 September, a company files financial statements in AOC-4 within 30 days, its annual return in MGT-7 or MGT-7A within 60 days, and the auditor appointment in ADT-1 within 15 days where applicable. Late AOC-4 and MGT-7 filings attract an additional fee of ₹100 a day each.

What happens if a company files its ITR late?

A late fee of up to ₹5,000 under Section 234F, interest under Section 234A on unpaid tax, the loss of the right to carry forward losses, and in serious cases prosecution under Section 276CC. A belated return can be filed until 31 December 2026.

Does the Income-tax Act, 2025 change company ITR filing for FY 2025-26?

Returns for FY 2025-26 are filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to income from tax year 2026-27 and renumbers provisions such as the concessional company tax regime and MAT, so plan next year's computation with the new references.
