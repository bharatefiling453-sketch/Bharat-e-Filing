Overview

What is GST return filing?

GST return filing is how a registered business reports its sales, purchases, tax collected and input tax credit to the government. Regular taxpayers file GSTR-1 (sales) and GSTR-3B (summary and tax payment) monthly, or quarterly under QRMP, plus GSTR-9 annually if turnover exceeds ₹2 crore. Late filing attracts late fees and 18% interest.

Returns are filed under Sections 37, 39 and 44 of the CGST Act, 2017 and Rules 59 to 81 of the CGST Rules. Even a month with no sales needs a nil return; skipping it costs late fees and can block your e-way bills and registration.

How your input tax credit flows

  1. GSTR-1Your supplier reports the sale
  2. IMSYou accept, reject or keep it pending
  3. GSTR-2BAccepted credit appears on the 14th
  4. GSTR-3BYou claim ITC and pay tax

The Invoice Management System (IMS) lets you act on every invoice your suppliers report before it reaches GSTR-2B. Rejecting a wrong invoice protects you from claiming credit you are not entitled to. Credit for a year must be claimed by 30 November of the following year or the annual return date, whichever is earlier (Section 16(4)).

What’s new

Latest GST return changes, 2025–26

Every change that affects how you file, newest first. We update this timeline as GSTN and CBIC issue notices.

  1. 1 April 2026

    Income-tax Act, 2025 in force

    The new Act replaces the 1961 Act. GST returns are unchanged, but your GST turnover is matched with the new ITR forms (section mapping below).

  2. 29 October 2025

    Returns barred after three years Act now

    GSTN advised taxpayers to file pending returns: GSTR-1, 1A, 3B, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 9C can no longer be filed three years after their due date (Sections 37, 39, 44 and 52, as amended by the Finance Act, 2023).

  3. 22 September 2025

    GST 2.0 rate rationalisation

    Main slabs are now 5% and 18%, plus 40% for specified goods. Invoices, HSN summaries and GSTR-1 must reflect the new rates from this date.

  4. From the July 2025 tax period

    GSTR-3B sales figures locked Changed

    Tax liability auto-filled into GSTR-3B from GSTR-1, GSTR-1A or IFF can no longer be edited. Fix mistakes through GSTR-1A before you file GSTR-3B.

  5. FY 2024-25 onwards

    GSTR-9 relief up to ₹2 crore

    The annual return is optional for aggregate turnover up to ₹2 crore (Notification 15/2025-CT). GSTR-9C still applies above ₹5 crore.

  6. Since October 2024

    Invoice Management System (IMS)

    Accept, reject or keep supplier invoices pending before they reach GSTR-2B. GSTN has been tightening how IMS actions feed GSTR-2B and GSTR-3B; we follow every advisory so your credit is never at risk.

  • GSTR-1 blocked: you cannot file GSTR-1 if the previous period’s GSTR-3B is pending (Rule 59(6)).

Worked example

A Lucknow retailer with ₹80 lakh turnover files the June GSTR-3B 30 days late with ₹50,000 tax to pay in cash. Late fee: 30 × ₹50 = ₹1,500 (under the ₹2,000 cap). Interest: ₹50,000 × 18% × 30/365 = ₹740. That’s ₹2,240 for one late month, before counting blocked e-way bills and unhappy buyers.

Annual return

GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C

The annual return consolidates the year’s GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B. It is mandatory above ₹2 crore aggregate turnover and due by 31 December after the financial year. Above ₹5 crore, you also file GSTR-9C, a self-certified reconciliation of your GST returns with audited accounts.

FY 2025-26 needs extra care

GST rates changed mid-year on 22 September 2025, so the annual return must reconcile sales at old and new rates separately.

GST × Income tax

GST returns and the Income-tax Act, 2025

The income-tax department compares the turnover in your GST returns with your income-tax return. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act, so the sections you will see on notices and in your ITR have changed.

FAQs

Questions? Answered.

What is GST return filing?

GST return filing is how a registered business reports its sales, purchases, tax collected and input tax credit to the government. Regular taxpayers file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B monthly or quarterly, composition dealers file CMP-08 and GSTR-4, and businesses above ₹2 crore turnover also file the annual return GSTR-9.

What are the due dates for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

Monthly filers must file GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th of the next month. Under the QRMP scheme (turnover up to ₹5 crore), GSTR-1 is due by the 13th of the month after the quarter and GSTR-3B by the 22nd or 24th depending on your state, with tax paid monthly through PMT-06 by the 25th.

What is the late fee for GSTR-3B?

The late fee is ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST), or ₹20 per day for a nil return. It is capped at ₹2,000 for turnover up to ₹1.5 crore, ₹5,000 up to ₹5 crore and ₹10,000 above that; nil returns are capped at ₹500 (Notification 19/2021-Central Tax). Interest at 18% a year is charged separately on tax paid late.

Do I need to file GST returns if there are no sales?

Yes. A nil GSTR-1 and nil GSTR-3B must still be filed for every period, and they can even be filed by SMS. Skipping them attracts late fees, blocks e-way bills and can lead to cancellation of your registration.

What is IMS and how does it affect my input tax credit?

The Invoice Management System shows every invoice your suppliers report in GSTR-1 or IFF. You can accept, reject or keep each one pending; accepted or deemed-accepted invoices flow into GSTR-2B, which is the basis for the input tax credit you claim in GSTR-3B.

Can I edit sales figures in GSTR-3B?

No. From the July 2025 tax period, tax liability auto-filled in GSTR-3B from GSTR-1, GSTR-1A or IFF cannot be edited. Correct any error by filing GSTR-1A for the same period before you file GSTR-3B.

Can I file GST returns that are years old?

Only within three years of the original due date. The Finance Act, 2023 amended Sections 37, 39, 44 and 52 so that returns cannot be filed after three years, and GSTN's advisory of 29 October 2025 confirms such returns are barred on the GST portal.

What happens if I do not file GSTR-3B?

You pay late fees and 18% interest, you cannot file the next GSTR-1 (Rule 59(6)), e-way bill generation is blocked after two missed periods (Rule 138E), your buyers cannot claim credit, and continuous non-filing can lead to cancellation of registration under Section 29(2)(c).

What is the QRMP scheme and who can opt for it?

QRMP lets taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly while paying tax monthly through PMT-06 by the 25th. B2B invoices can be uploaded monthly through IFF so customers get their credit on time.

What is the interest rate on late GST payment?

Interest is 18% a year on tax paid late in cash, from the day after the due date (Section 50(1)). Input tax credit wrongly availed and utilised attracts 24% a year (Section 50(3)).

Who must file GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C?

GSTR-9 is mandatory for regular taxpayers with aggregate turnover above ₹2 crore and is due by 31 December after the financial year. Taxpayers above ₹5 crore must also file GSTR-9C, a self-certified reconciliation statement.

Do composition dealers file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

No. Composition dealers file CMP-08 by the 18th after each quarter to pay tax, and the annual return GSTR-4 by 30 April after the financial year.

How do GST returns connect with the Income-tax Act, 2025?

The income-tax department matches the turnover in your GST returns with your income-tax return. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies: presumptive income moves from sections 44AD/44ADA/44AE to section 58, tax audit from section 44AB to section 63, and TDS sections such as 194Q, 194C and 194J are consolidated in section 393.
