# ROC Filing — 7 Meta Static Ad Prompts (Bharat eFiling)

Format for all: Meta feed 4:5, 1080×1350 px. Keep the main message in the centre 80% (Meta crops edges on some placements).
Offer on every ad: **Free ROC status + penalty check on WhatsApp** (the lead sends their CIN or LLPIN).
Facts used: AOC-4 due 29 Oct 2026 · LLP Form 8 due 30 Oct 2026 · MGT-7/7A due 28 Nov 2026 · late fee ₹100/day per form, no upper cap · 3 years without filing means directors can be disqualified (Sec 164(2)) and the company can be struck off.
Never use: MCA or Government logos, "guaranteed", anything that looks like an official notice.

## 01 — "The Penalty Meter" (dark fintech dashboard)
**Prompt:** A dark-mode fintech app screen on a deep graphite background (#0B0F14) with a soft green-to-red glow behind it. In the centre, a frosted-glass card shows a giant odometer-style counter "₹18,000" in a monospaced tabular font, with the label "AOC-4 • 180 din late" and a small red line chart climbing steeply. A tiny pulsing red dot reads "LIVE · +₹100/day". Headline above in bold white sans: "Yeh meter roz ₹100 badhta hai." Supporting line: "AOC-4, MGT-7, LLP Form 8 — har form par. Koi upper limit nahi." CTA pill at the bottom in mint green: "Free penalty check → WhatsApp". Small "Bharat eFiling · 11+ years · CA & CS team" footer. Clean, premium, Apple-widget glassmorphism.
- **Emotion:** loss aversion (money is leaking right now) · **Audience:** Pvt Ltd directors · **Uses a date:** No

## 02 — "The Sticky Note" (lo-fi, looks like a real desk)
**Prompt:** Top-down flat-lay of an Indian business owner's wooden desk: corner of a laptop, a steel chai glass, a calculator and a pen. In the centre, a slightly tilted bright yellow Post-it with handwritten blue ink: "AOC-4 → 29 Oct!!", "MGT-7 → 28 Nov", with an underline and a doodled circle. A small caption strip at the bottom, white text on black: "Yaad tha? 🙂 Na bhi ho — hum file kar denge." Tiny brand tag in the corner. Should look like a native photo, not an ad.
- **Emotion:** relatable "oops, I forgot" · **Audience:** busy small-company owners · **Uses a date:** Yes (the dates are the message)

**Result:** ✅ No two ads share a format, palette or emotion. Only 2 of 7 use a date (02 and 04, where the date is the message). 6 are for Pvt Ltd and 1 is LLP-specific. The ads cover four kinds of persuasion (fear, relief, trust, logic), so Meta has truly different concepts to test against each other.
