# FAST-DS 2026 — 7 Meta Static Ads for RSU / ESOP / ESPP Holders (Bharat eFiling)

**Format:** 4:5 feed, rendered in ultra HD at 2160×2700 (2× of 1080×1350).
**Audience:** salaried tech and MNC employees with RSUs, ESOPs or ESPP in a foreign brokerage; people back from onsite postings who still hold foreign accounts or a 401(k).
**Tone:** calm, smart, peer-to-peer. This is an *honest miss with a simple fix*, never "black money".
**Offer (no price shown):** a free, confidential check of which category they fall in. Fees are shared once a lead is interested.
**Proof line:** "Bharat eFiling · RSU & foreign-asset ITR specialists · 11+ yrs". No individual CA is shown, in line with ICAI ethics.

**Facts used, and only these:**
- FAST-DS 2026 window: 16 Aug – 31 Dec 2026.
- Category B: income already taxed (or asset bought while non-resident) but not reported in Schedule FA. Flat ₹1 lakh fee, for assets up to ₹5 crore.
- Category A: income never taxed. 60% of value, up to ₹1 crore.
- A valid declaration gives immunity from Black Money Act penalty and prosecution.
- RSUs, ESPP shares or a bank account held abroad are foreign assets that belong in Schedule FA.

**Never use:** government logos, anything that looks like the Income Tax Department's email, "black money / hide / amnesty for evaders" wording, or real employer or broker logos.

## Testing tips
- **Targeting:** Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, NCR, Chennai and Mumbai, ages 25–45, broad targeting. Let the hooks (RSU, ESPP, onsite) find the right people.
- **Lead route:** a Meta Instant Form with 3 questions works best here, because people prefer privacy (asset type, already taxed?, approximate value). Click-to-WhatsApp is the alternative.
- **Primary text:** keep it short and calm. For example: "RSUs, ESPP or an old onsite bank account count as foreign assets. If Schedule FA was missed, FAST-DS 2026 offers a one-time fix till 31 Dec. Get a free, confidential category check from RSU ITR specialists."
